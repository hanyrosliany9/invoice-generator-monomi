# Narasi / Narration: Inbox WhatsApp

Video: `crm-whatsapp-inbox.daa4e56c.mp4` (1280x800, 02:20.6, silent). Guide slug: `crm-whatsapp-inbox`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Baca dan jawab chat WhatsApp dari nomor bisnis langsung di Monomi. Setiap chat baru otomatis menjadi lead, lengkap dengan iklan asalnya.
- EN: Read and answer WhatsApp chats from the business number inside Monomi. Every new chat becomes a lead automatically, with the ad it came from.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka inbox

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.3 (9.3 s)
- ID (23 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buka inbox. Buka Marketing > CRM > Inbox. Daftar menampilkan setiap chat dengan tahap, kode kampanye, dan angka hijau untuk pesan belum dibaca.
- EN (27 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Open the inbox. Go to Marketing > CRM > Inbox. The list shows every chat with its stage, campaign code and a green number for unread messages.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Daftar diperbarui sendiri setiap beberapa detik. / The list refreshes by itself every few seconds.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Saring chat yang belum dibaca

- Window: 00:09.3 - 00:16.5 (7.2 s)
- ID (15 of max 16 words): Saring chat yang belum dibaca. Klik Belum dibaca untuk melihat hanya chat yang menunggu jawaban.
- EN (15 of max 17 words): Filter the unread chats. Click Unread to see only the chats waiting for an answer.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Milik saya menampilkan chat yang ditugaskan ke Anda; Belum ditugaskan menampilkan chat yang belum ada yang menangani. / Mine shows the chats assigned to you; Unassigned shows chats nobody has picked up.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Chat baru menjadi lead, lengkap dengan iklan asalnya

- Window: 00:16.5 - 00:27.8 (11.3 s)
- ID (22 of max 26 words): Chat baru menjadi lead, lengkap dengan iklan asalnya. Saat nomor yang belum dikenal menulis pertama kali, Monomi membuat lead di kolom Baru.
- EN (27 of max 28 words): New chats become leads, with the ad they came from. When an unknown number writes for the first time, Monomi creates a lead in the New column.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Isi ID iklan di kampanye agar iklan tanpa kode tetap tercocokkan. / Add the ad IDs to the campaign so ads without the code are still matched.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Balas dari Monomi

- Window: 00:27.8 - 00:43.2 (15.4 s)
- ID (27 of max 36 words): Balas dari Monomi. Buka chat, ketik di kolom lalu tekan Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter di Mac) atau tombol kirim. Nama Anda tampil di atas gelembung sebagai Monomi · nama.
- EN (31 of max 39 words): Reply from Monomi. Open a chat, type in the box and press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) or the send button. Your name appears above the bubble as Monomi · name.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Tekan Ctrl+Enter, bukan Enter saja: Enter membuat baris baru. / Press Ctrl+Enter, not Enter alone: Enter adds a new line.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Pakai balasan cepat

- Window: 00:43.3 - 00:55.1 (11.9 s)
- ID (31 of max 27 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Pakai balasan cepat. Tombol petir membuka balasan cepat Anda. Klik salah satu untuk memasukkan teksnya ke kolom, ubah bila perlu, lalu kirim. Jika Anda sudah mengetik sesuatu, balasan ditambahkan di bawahnya.
- EN (24 of max 29 words): Use quick replies. The lightning button opens your quick replies. Click one to put its text in the box, then edit it and send.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Balasan cepat diatur di CRM > Pengaturan. / Quick replies are managed in CRM > Settings.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Balasan dari HP tetap tercatat

- Window: 00:55.2 - 01:04.5 (9.3 s)
- ID (32 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Balasan dari HP tetap tercatat. Tim tetap bisa menjawab lewat aplikasi WhatsApp Business di HP. Balasan itu tampil di Monomi dengan label Dikirim dari aplikasi HP dan dihitung sebagai respons pertama lead.
- EN (19 of max 23 words): Replies from the phone still count. The team can keep answering in the WhatsApp Business app on the phone.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Jangan pernah mendaftarkan atau memverifikasi ulang nomor di Meta: aplikasi di HP akan keluar. / Never register or verify the number again in Meta: it would log the phone app out.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Jendela 24 jam

- Window: 01:04.5 - 01:16.8 (12.3 s)
- ID (30 of max 28 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Jendela 24 jam. Anda bebas menulis selama 24 jam setelah pesan terakhir pelanggan (garis hijau di atas kolom). Saat jendela tertutup, WhatsApp hanya mengizinkan template yang disetujui: klik Kirim template.
- EN (32 of max 30 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): The 24-hour window. You can write freely for 24 hours after the customer's last message (green line above the box). When it is closed WhatsApp only allows approved templates: click Send template.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Chat dari iklan juga menampilkan jendela gratis (72 jam). / Chats from an ad also show the free-entry window (72 hours).

## Langkah 8 / Step 8: Kirim template

- Window: 01:16.8 - 01:29.1 (12.3 s)
- ID (22 of max 28 words): Kirim template. Pilih template yang disetujui, lalu isi variabelnya, Variabel 1 dan Variabel 2. Pratinjau menampilkan pesan dengan isian Anda. Klik Kirim.
- EN (29 of max 30 words): Send a template. Choose an approved template, then fill in its variables, Variable 1 and Variable 2. The preview shows the message with your values filled in. Click Send.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Template dibuat dan disetujui di WhatsApp Manager milik Meta, bukan di Monomi. / Templates are created and approved in Meta's WhatsApp Manager, not in Monomi.

## Langkah 9 / Step 9: Tugaskan, hubungkan, arsipkan

- Window: 01:29.1 - 01:43.5 (14.4 s)
- ID (28 of max 33 words): Tugaskan, hubungkan, arsipkan. Pakai menu di kepala chat untuk menugaskan chat ke rekan, tombol tautan untuk menghubungkannya ke lead lain, dan tombol arsip untuk menyembunyikan chat yang selesai.
- EN (35 of max 36 words): Assign, link and archive. Use the menu in the chat header to assign the chat to a colleague, the link button to connect it to another lead, and the archive button to hide finished chats.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Chat yang ditugaskan menampilkan nama rekan di daftar. / Assigned chats show the colleague's name in the list.

## Langkah 10 / Step 10: Lihat chat di halaman lead

- Window: 01:43.5 - 01:57.8 (14.2 s)
- ID (32 of max 33 words): Lihat chat di halaman lead. Di halaman lead, buka tab WhatsApp untuk membaca dan menjawab percakapan yang sama tanpa meninggalkan lead. Balasan, centang, dan jendela 24 jam bekerja persis seperti di inbox.
- EN (25 of max 35 words): See the chat on the lead. On a lead page, open the WhatsApp tab to read and answer the same conversation without leaving the lead.

## Langkah 11 / Step 11: Di HP

- Window: 01:57.8 - 02:07.0 (9.2 s)
- ID (31 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Di HP. Di HP, inbox menampilkan daftar selebar layar, dengan angka belum dibaca, tahap, dan kampanye di setiap baris. Ketuk chat untuk membukanya dan panah di atas untuk kembali ke daftar.
- EN (22 of max 22 words): On a phone. On a phone the inbox shows the list full width, with unread numbers, stage and campaign on every row.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Pasang Monomi di layar utama HP agar inbox terbuka dalam satu ketukan. / You can install Monomi on your phone's home screen to reach the inbox in one tap.

## Langkah 12 / Step 12: Balasan cepat dan pengaturan

- Window: 02:07.1 - 02:20.6 (13.5 s)
- ID (30 of max 31 words): Balasan cepat dan pengaturan. CRM > Pengaturan berisi editor balasan cepat. Klik Tambah balasan cepat, beri judul dan teks, lalu tekan Simpan. Halaman yang sama menunjukkan apakah WhatsApp sudah terhubung.
- EN (26 of max 34 words): Quick replies and settings. CRM > Settings has the quick replies editor. Click Add quick reply, give it a title and the text and press Save.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Hanya admin yang bisa melihat inbox dan pengaturannya. Cara menyiapkan koneksi ada di panduan Koneksi WhatsApp (admin). / Only admins can see the inbox and the settings. How the connection is set up is in the WhatsApp connection (admin) guide.
