# Narasi / Narration: Aset dan penyusutan

Video: `aset-penyusutan.deab8184.mp4` (1280x820, 01:15.7, silent). Guide slug: `aset-penyusutan`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Catat peralatan produksi sebagai aset tetap, atur umur ekonomisnya, dan jalankan penyusutan bulanan sesuai PSAK 16.
- EN: Record production equipment as fixed assets, set the useful life, and run the monthly depreciation according to PSAK 16.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka menu Aset

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.4 (9.4 s)
- ID (28 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buka menu Aset. Manajemen Aset memuat semua peralatan: kamera, lensa, lampu, dan lainnya, beserta statusnya. Klik Aset Baru untuk menambah, atau cari berdasarkan kode, nama, model, atau serial.
- EN (31 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Open the Aset menu. Manajemen Aset lists all equipment: cameras, lenses, lights and more, with their status. Click Aset Baru to add one, or search by code, name, model or serial.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Isi identitas aset

- Window: 00:09.4 - 00:29.4 (20.0 s)
- ID (22 of max 47 words): Isi identitas aset. Isi Nama Aset dan pilih Kategori. Kode aset dibuat otomatis. Lengkapi merek, model, dan nomor seri untuk klaim garansi.
- EN (28 of max 50 words): Fill in the asset identity. Enter the Nama Aset and choose the Kategori. The asset code is generated automatically. Add manufacturer, model and serial number for warranty claims.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Isi data pembelian

- Window: 00:29.4 - 00:39.5 (10.1 s)
- ID (24 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Isi data pembelian. Isi Tanggal Pembelian dan Harga Pembelian, lalu pilih Sumber Dana: Cash, Bank Transfer, atau Credit. Supplier dan nomor invoice bersifat opsional.
- EN (28 of max 25 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Fill in the purchase data. Enter the Tanggal Pembelian and Harga Pembelian, then choose the Sumber Dana: Cash, Bank Transfer or Credit. Supplier and invoice number are optional.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Pembelian aset membuat jurnal otomatis dari sumber dana yang dipilih. / Buying an asset creates a journal automatically from the chosen source of funds.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Atur penyusutan

- Window: 00:39.5 - 00:50.8 (11.3 s)
- ID (20 of max 26 words): Atur penyusutan. Pilih Kelompok Penyusutan; umur ekonomis terisi menurut kelompoknya dan bisa disesuaikan. Isi Nilai Sisa bila ada. Klik Simpan.
- EN (27 of max 28 words): Set the depreciation. Choose the Kelompok Penyusutan; the useful life is filled in by group and can be adjusted. Enter the Nilai Sisa if any. Click Simpan.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Penyusutan memakai metode garis lurus sesuai PSAK 16. / Depreciation uses the straight-line method according to PSAK 16.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Lihat halaman aset

- Window: 00:50.8 - 01:03.2 (12.4 s)
- ID (25 of max 28 words): Lihat halaman aset. Setelah disimpan, halaman aset memuat nilai perolehan, nilai buku, dan Skema Penyusutan dengan penyusutan per bulan. Tombol Proses Penyusutan membuka halaman penyusutan.
- EN (30 of max 31 words): View the asset page. After saving, the asset page shows the acquisition value, book value and the Skema Penyusutan with monthly depreciation. The Proses Penyusutan button opens the depreciation page.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Aset juga bisa dipinjam, direservasi, dan dicatat perawatannya dari halaman ini. / Assets can also be borrowed, reserved and have maintenance logged from this page.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Jalankan penyusutan bulanan

- Window: 01:03.2 - 01:15.7 (12.5 s)
- ID (25 of max 28 words): Jalankan penyusutan bulanan. Di menu Akuntansi, Penyusutan Aset, atur periode lalu klik Proses Depresiasi. Total Akumulasi dan nilai buku diperbarui, dan jurnal penyusutan dibuat otomatis.
- EN (30 of max 31 words): Run monthly depreciation. In menu Akuntansi, Penyusutan Aset, set the period and click Proses Depresiasi. Total Akumulasi and the book value are updated, and the depreciation journal is created automatically.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Proses sekali per bulan, setelah semua aset yang dibeli bulan itu tercatat. / Run it once a month, after all assets bought that month are recorded.
