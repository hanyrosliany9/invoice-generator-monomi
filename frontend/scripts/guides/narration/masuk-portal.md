Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Masuk dengan kode 6 digit yang dikirim ke email Anda, tanpa kata sandi.
- EN: Sign in with the 6-digit code sent to your email, no password needed.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Masukkan email Anda

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:07.4 (7.4 s)
- ID (12 of max 16 words): Masukkan email Anda. Ketik email yang didaftarkan Monomi, lalu ketuk Kirim kode.
- EN (14 of max 18 words): Enter your email. Type the email Monomi has on file, then tap Send code.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Email harus yang sama dengan yang didaftarkan; jika belum, hubungi tim Monomi. / It must be the registered email; if not, contact the Monomi team.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Masukkan kode 6 digit

- Window: 00:07.4 - 00:17.9 (10.5 s)
- ID (19 of max 24 words): Masukkan kode 6 digit. Buka email Anda, salin kode 6 digit, dan ketik di kolom. Kode berlaku beberapa menit.
- EN (25 of max 26 words): Enter the 6-digit code. Open your email, copy the 6-digit code and type it in the field. The code is valid for a few minutes.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Tidak ada email? Periksa folder spam atau ketuk Kirim ulang kode. / No email? Check your spam folder or tap Resend code.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Pilih klien

- Window: 00:17.9 - 00:24.6 (6.7 s)
- ID (12 of max 15 words): Pilih klien. Bila akun Anda terhubung ke beberapa klien, ketuk salah satunya.
- EN (15 of max 16 words): Choose a client. If your account is linked to several clients, tap one of them.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Kenali tampilan portal

- Window: 00:24.6 - 00:34.8 (10.2 s)
- ID (22 of max 23 words): Kenali tampilan portal. Empat tab mengatur isi: Konten, Laporan, Media, dan Deck. Ganti klien bila Anda punya akses ke lebih dari satu.
- EN (24 of max 25 words): Get to know the portal. Four tabs organise it: Content, Reports, Media and Decks. Switch client if you have access to more than one.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Tombol EN/ID mengganti bahasa. / The EN/ID button changes the language.
