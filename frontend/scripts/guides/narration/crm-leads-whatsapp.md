# Narasi / Narration: CRM: lead dari iklan WhatsApp

Video: `crm-leads-whatsapp.7211b213.mp4` (1280x800, 02:46.4, silent). Guide slug: `crm-leads-whatsapp`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Lacak setiap orang yang chat dari iklan WhatsApp: lihat dari kampanye mana mereka datang, balas dengan cepat, tindak lanjuti, lalu ubah menjadi klien dan penawaran.
- EN: Track every person who messages you from a WhatsApp ad: see which campaign they came from, reply fast, follow up, and turn them into a client and quotation.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buat kampanye untuk setiap iklan

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:11.4 (11.4 s)
- ID (31 of max 26 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buat kampanye untuk setiap iklan. Buka Marketing > CRM > Kampanye. Kampanye dari iklan Meta Anda muncul sendiri setelah sinkronisasi pertama, dengan kode diambil dari nama kampanye iklan (kode boleh diganti).
- EN (12 of max 28 words): Create a campaign for each ad. Open Marketing > CRM > Campaigns.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Iklan WhatsApp (click-to-WhatsApp) tetap memerlukan kodenya di pesan otomatis di bawah. Pakai satu kode per kampanye iklan supaya dasbor bisa membandingkan dengan adil. / A WhatsApp (click-to-WhatsApp) ad still needs its code in the pre-filled message below. Use one code per ad campaign so the dashboard can compare fairly.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Salin pesan iklan beserta kode kampanye

- Window: 00:11.4 - 00:20.9 (9.4 s)
- ID (18 of max 21 words): Salin pesan iklan beserta kode kampanye. Pilih kampanye. Kotak menampilkan pesan otomatis dengan kode di akhir, misalnya [FB-OKT1].
- EN (26 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Copy the ad message with the campaign code. Select the campaign. The box shows the pre-filled message with the code at the end, for example [FB-OKT1].
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Jangan hapus kode dari pesan. Chat tanpa kode hanya tercocokkan jika ID iklan sudah diisi di kampanye. / Do not delete the code from the message. Chats without it are only matched if you add the ad ID to the campaign.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Iklan website: satu link untuk semua iklan

- Window: 00:20.9 - 00:30.8 (9.9 s)
- ID (24 of max 22 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Iklan website: satu link untuk semua iklan. Jika iklan mengarahkan orang ke landing page dulu, pilih kampanye mana saja lalu klik Salin link iklan.
- EN (25 of max 24 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Website ads: one link for every ad. If the ad sends people to the landing page first, select any campaign and click Copy ad link.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Jangan ubah parameternya: Meta mengisi id kampanye dan id iklan dengan sendirinya. / Do not change the parameters: Meta fills in the campaign id and the ad id by itself.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Biaya iklan tersinkron dari Meta

- Window: 00:30.8 - 00:41.9 (11.1 s)
- ID (15 of max 25 words): Biaya iklan tersinkron dari Meta. ) dan setiap harinya berlabel Meta di daftar biaya kampanye.
- EN (18 of max 27 words): Ad spend syncs from Meta. ) and each day shows a Meta label in the campaign's spend list.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Catatan Anda bisa dihapus dengan ikon tempat sampah; hari-hari dari Meta tidak bisa diubah. Jika angka terlihat lama, admin bisa klik Sinkronkan sekarang di pengaturan CRM. / Your own entries can be deleted with the bin icon; synced Meta days cannot be edited. If the numbers look old, an admin can click Sync now in CRM settings.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Tambah lead dalam hitungan detik

- Window: 00:41.9 - 00:57.7 (15.8 s)
- ID (32 of max 37 words): Tambah lead dalam hitungan detik. Saat ada yang chat, klik Lead baru, atau tekan Ctrl+Shift+L (Cmd+Shift+L di Mac) di halaman mana pun. Tempel pesan pertamanya: nama, nomor, dan kode kampanye terdeteksi sendiri.
- EN (22 of max 40 words): Add a lead in seconds. When someone messages you, click New lead, or press Ctrl+Shift+L (Cmd+Shift+L on a Mac) on any page.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Jika nomor sudah ada, muncul peringatan dengan tautan ke lead yang sudah ada. Simpan & tambah lagi membiarkan jendela tetap terbuka untuk lead berikutnya. / If the number already exists you get a warning with a link to the existing lead. Save & add another keeps the window open for the next one.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Chat dari landing page membawa Kode

- Window: 00:57.7 - 01:09.8 (12.1 s)
- ID (23 of max 27 words): Chat dari landing page membawa Kode. Chat yang berawal dari landing page diakhiri baris seperti Kode: K7QM2X dan sering ada baris Instagram: @nama.
- EN (29 of max 30 words): Chats from the landing page carry a Kode. A chat that starts from the landing page ends with a line like Kode: K7QM2X and often an Instagram: @name line.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Satu kode hanya bisa dihubungkan ke satu lead. Tanpa kode, lead tetap tersimpan, hanya saja tidak bisa dilaporkan ke Meta sebagai lead website. / A code links to one lead only. Without it the lead is still saved, it just cannot report back to Meta as a website lead.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Lead yang menunggu WhatsApp

- Window: 01:09.8 - 01:23.6 (13.8 s)
- ID (31 of max 32 words): Lead yang menunggu WhatsApp. Saat seseorang menekan Lanjut ke WhatsApp di form landing page, lead langsung muncul di papan dengan label Menunggu WhatsApp. Pertanyaan di form (Instagram, brand, kategori) sifatnya opsional.
- EN (30 of max 34 words): Leads that wait for WhatsApp. When someone taps Lanjut ke WhatsApp on the landing page form, a lead appears on the board at once with the badge Waiting for WhatsApp.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Lead yang menunggu tidak dihitung sebagai belum dibalas dan tidak masuk angka di samping Lead pada menu. Jika tidak ada pesan dalam 30 hari, lead pindah ke Kalah (Tidak pernah mengirim WhatsApp). / Waiting leads do not count as unanswered and are not in the number next to Leads in the menu. If no message arrives within 30 days, the lead moves to Lost (Never sent WhatsApp).

## Langkah 8 / Step 8: Kerjakan lewat papan

- Window: 01:23.6 - 01:36.6 (13.0 s)
- ID (29 of max 30 words): Kerjakan lewat papan. Lead berada di kolom: Baru, Dikualifikasi, Meeting, Penawaran, Menang, Kalah. Seret kartu ke kolom lain. Kartu oranye belum dibalas dalam target waktu respons, dan tombol menyaringnya.
- EN (19 of max 32 words): Work the board. Leads sit in columns: New, Qualified, Meeting, Proposal, Won, Lost. Drag a card to another column.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Di HP tekan dan tahan kartu untuk menyeretnya, atau pakai tombol pindah di kartu. / On a phone press and hold a card to drag it, or use the move button on the card.

## Langkah 9 / Step 9: Tampilan daftar dan aksi massal

- Window: 01:36.6 - 01:45.4 (8.8 s)
- ID (19 of max 20 words): Tampilan daftar dan aksi massal. Pindah ke Daftar untuk tabel berisi tahap, kampanye, penanggung jawab, tindak lanjut, dan estimasi.
- EN (18 of max 21 words): List view and bulk actions. Switch to List for a table with stage, campaign, owner, follow-up and estimate.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Kolom pencarian bisa mencari nama, nomor, atau kode kampanye. / The search box finds a name, a number or a campaign code.

## Langkah 10 / Step 10: Jadwalkan tindak lanjut

- Window: 01:45.4 - 01:56.3 (10.9 s)
- ID (22 of max 25 words): Jadwalkan tindak lanjut. Buka lead. Jadwalkan tindak lanjut memasang pengingat beserta catatan pada lead; tandai selesai atau jadwalkan ulang saat waktunya tiba.
- EN (26 of max 27 words): Schedule follow-ups. Open a lead. Schedule follow-up puts a reminder with a note on the lead; mark it done or reschedule it when the time comes.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Tindak lanjut yang terlambat membuat kartu di papan berwarna oranye. / A follow-up that is overdue turns the card amber on the board.

## Langkah 11 / Step 11: Pindahkan lead ke tahap lain

- Window: 01:56.3 - 02:08.0 (11.7 s)
- ID (26 of max 27 words): Pindahkan lead ke tahap lain. Klik Pindah tahap lalu pilih tahap baru. Hanya dua tahap yang dilaporkan ke Meta: Dikualifikasi (QualifiedLead) dan Menang (Purchase, beserta nilainya).
- EN (29 of max 29 words): Move a lead to another stage. Click Move stage and choose the new stage. Only two stages are reported to Meta: Qualified (QualifiedLead) and Won (Purchase, with the value).
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Saat penawaran disetujui atau invoice dibayar, lead pindah ke Menang dengan sendirinya dan mengirim Purchase. / When the quotation is approved or the invoice is paid, the lead moves to Won on its own and sends Purchase.

## Langkah 12 / Step 12: Konversi menjadi klien dan penawaran

- Window: 02:08.0 - 02:19.3 (11.3 s)
- ID (24 of max 26 words): Konversi menjadi klien dan penawaran. Saat lead siap, klik Konversi ke Klien and Buat Penawaran. Biarkan Buat draf penawaran tercentang, periksa nilainya, lalu konfirmasi.
- EN (27 of max 28 words): Convert to client and quotation. When the lead is ready, click Convert to Client and Create Quotation. Keep Create draft quotation ticked, check the amount and confirm.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Konversi (atau memindahkan lead ke Menang) otomatis mengirim sinyal Dikualifikasi (QualifiedLead) ke Meta bila belum pernah terkirim, jadi tidak perlu memindahkan lead ke Dikualifikasi lebih dulu. Tahap Penawaran sendiri tidak mengirim apa pun. Hilangkan centang penawaran jika hanya butuh klien dan proyek. / Converting (or moving the lead to Won) automatically sends the Qualified signal (QualifiedLead) to Meta if it was not sent yet, so there is no need to move the lead to Qualified first. Proposal itself sends nothing. Untick the quotation if you only want the client and the project.

## Langkah 13 / Step 13: Apa yang dibuat oleh konversi

- Window: 02:19.3 - 02:32.2 (12.9 s)
- ID (29 of max 30 words): Apa yang dibuat oleh konversi. Jendela ini berisi tautan ke Klien, Proyek, dan Draf penawaran yang baru. Lead pindah ke Penawaran dan tautan yang sama muncul di Info lead.
- EN (28 of max 32 words): What the conversion created. The window links to the new Client, Project and Draft quotation. The lead moves to Proposal and the same links appear under Lead info.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Riwayat mencatat konversi dan penawarannya, dalam bahasa Anda. / The History records the conversion and the quotation, in your language.

## Langkah 14 / Step 14: Baca dasbor

- Window: 02:32.2 - 02:46.4 (14.2 s)
- ID (48 of max 33 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Baca dasbor. Buka Dasbor CRM: lead baru, klien menang, pendapatan, biaya iklan (sinkron dari Meta ditambah biaya lain), biaya per lead, dan biaya per klien, corong penjualan, kecepatan respons, serta tabel per kampanye berisi lead, pendapatan, biaya, biaya per lead, biaya per lead Qualified, dan biaya per klien.
- EN (52 of max 35 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Read the dashboard. Open CRM Dashboard: new leads, clients won, revenue, ad spend (synced from Meta plus other costs), cost per lead and cost per client, the sales funnel, the response speed, and a table per campaign with leads, revenue, spend, cost per lead, cost per Qualified lead and cost per client.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Kecepatan respons dibandingkan dengan target yang Anda atur di pengaturan CRM. / Response speed is compared with the target you set in CRM settings.
