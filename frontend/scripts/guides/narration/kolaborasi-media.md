# Narasi / Narration: Kolaborasi media

Video: `kolaborasi-media.e8c4510c.mp4` (1280x900, 01:19.4, silent). Guide slug: `kolaborasi-media`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Kumpulkan foto dan video hasil produksi, tinjau bersama klien, dan bagikan lewat tautan.
- EN: Collect photos and videos from a shoot, review them with the client and share them by link.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buat proyek media

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.2 (9.2 s)
- ID (16 of max 21 words): Buat proyek media. Di Kolaborasi Media klik Proyek Baru, isi nama proyek, lalu klik Buat Proyek.
- EN (18 of max 22 words): Create a media project. In Media Collaboration click New Project, enter a project name, then click Create Project.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Hubungkan ke klien dan proyek agar klien melihatnya di portal. / Link it to the client and project so the client sees it in the portal.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Unggah aset

- Window: 00:09.2 - 00:18.2 (9.0 s)
- ID (19 of max 20 words): Unggah aset. Buka proyek, lalu klik Unggah Aset. Pilih banyak foto dan video sekaligus; progres unggah tampil di layar.
- EN (21 of max 22 words): Upload assets. Open the project, then click Upload Asset. Pick many photos and videos at once; upload progress shows on screen.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Rapikan dengan folder

- Window: 00:18.2 - 00:28.3 (10.1 s)
- ID (19 of max 23 words): Rapikan dengan folder. Panel Folder di kiri galeri. Klik ikon tambah untuk membuat folder baru; Semua File menampilkan semuanya.
- EN (27 of max 25 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Keep things tidy with folders. The Folder panel is at the left of the gallery. Click the add icon to create a folder; All Files shows everything.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Pilih aset lalu unduh

- Window: 00:28.3 - 00:40.0 (11.8 s)
- ID (27 of max 27 words): Pilih aset lalu unduh. Klik Pilih pada aset, atau Pilih Semua. Bilah di bawah menyediakan Pindahkan, Beri nilai, Unduh, dan Hapus. Unduh Semua mengarsipkan seluruh aset proyek.
- EN (31 of max 29 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Select assets, then download. Click Select on an asset, or Select All. The bar at the bottom offers Move, Rate, Download and Delete. Download All archives every asset in the project.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Di ponsel: bilah pilihan di bawah layar

- Window: 00:40.1 - 00:51.4 (11.3 s)
- ID (25 of max 26 words): Di ponsel: bilah pilihan di bawah layar. Setelah memilih, bilah tindakan muncul menempel di bawah layar dengan ikon pindah folder, bandingkan, rating, unduh, dan hapus.
- EN (25 of max 28 words): On a phone: the selection bar. After selecting, the action bar sticks to the bottom of the screen with move, compare, rating, download and delete.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Beri rating dan komentar

- Window: 00:51.5 - 01:07.7 (16.2 s)
- ID (23 of max 37 words): Beri rating dan komentar. Klik aset untuk membukanya. Beri bintang dan tulis komentar di panel kanan; tim dan klien melihat masukan yang sama.
- EN (28 of max 41 words): Rate and comment. Click an asset to open it. Give stars and write a comment in the right panel; the team and the client see the same feedback.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Atur tautan publik

- Window: 01:07.7 - 01:19.4 (11.7 s)
- ID (23 of max 27 words): Atur tautan publik. Klik Atur Berbagi. Salin tautan untuk dikirim ke klien tanpa login, atur masa berlaku, atau Nonaktifkan bila tidak lagi dibutuhkan.
- EN (28 of max 29 words): Manage the public link. Click Manage Sharing. Copy the link to send to the client without login, set an expiry, or Deactivate when it is no longer needed.
