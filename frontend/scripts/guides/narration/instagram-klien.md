# Narasi / Narration: Instagram klien: hubungkan, sinkron, dan laporan

Video: `instagram-klien.97f5d3aa.mp4` (1280x900, 01:04.1, silent). Guide slug: `instagram-klien`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Hubungkan akun Instagram Bisnis atau Kreator milik klien dari halaman klien, pahami apa yang disinkronkan dan kapan, isi laporan bulanan dari datanya, dan putuskan koneksinya bila perlu.
- EN: Connect a client's own Instagram Business or Creator account from the client page, understand what is synced and when, fill the monthly report from that data, and disconnect when needed.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka kartu Instagram di halaman klien

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:10.1 (10.1 s)
- ID (22 of max 23 words): Buka kartu Instagram di halaman klien. Buka Klien, pilih klien, lalu gulir ke kartu Instagram. Selama belum terhubung kartu berbunyi Belum terhubung.
- EN (29 of max 25 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Open the Instagram card on the client page. Open Clients, pick the client, then scroll to the Instagram card. While nothing is connected it says Belum terhubung (Not connected).
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Akunnya harus Bisnis atau Kreator. Bila kartu menyebut integrasi belum dikonfigurasi di server, minta admin atau developer mengisi META_APP_ID dan META_APP_SECRET. / The account must be Business or Creator. If the card says the integration is not configured on the server, ask an admin or developer to set META_APP_ID and META_APP_SECRET.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Baca kartu yang sudah terhubung

- Window: 00:10.1 - 00:17.4 (7.3 s)
- ID (12 of max 16 words): Baca kartu yang sudah terhubung. Label di pojok kartu menunjukkan status koneksi.
- EN (13 of max 17 words): Read the connected card. The label in the corner shows the connection status.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Token Instagram diperbarui otomatis saat sinkronisasi harian ketika sudah mendekati batas, jadi Anda tidak perlu berbuat apa-apa selama statusnya Terhubung. / The Instagram token is renewed automatically during the daily sync when it gets close to its limit, so you do nothing as long as the status is Terhubung.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Apa yang disinkronkan, dan kapan

- Window: 00:17.4 - 00:28.6 (11.1 s)
- ID (35 of max 25 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Apa yang disinkronkan, dan kapan. 00 WIB: statistik akun per hari (jangkauan, tayangan, akun yang berinteraksi, interaksi, suka, komentar, dibagikan, disimpan, balasan, repost, ketukan tautan profil), jumlah pengikut, dan statistik tiap konten feed dan Reels.
- EN (41 of max 27 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): What is synced, and when. The system syncs every day at 02:00 WIB: daily account statistics (reach, views, accounts engaged, interactions, likes, comments, shares, saves, replies, reposts, profile link taps), the follower count, and statistics for each feed post and Reel.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Sync sekarang punya jeda minimal 2 menit dan hanya boleh 3 kali dalam 10 menit. Bila Instagram menolak satu metrik, sinkronisasi tetap berjalan dan metriknya disebut di kotak peringatan pada kartu. / Sync sekarang has a 2 minute minimum gap and allows only 3 runs per 10 minutes. If Instagram rejects one metric the sync still runs and the metric is named in the warning box on the card.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Arti status koneksi

- Window: 00:28.6 - 00:41.6 (13.0 s)
- ID (29 of max 30 words): Arti status koneksi. Terhubung: sinkronisasi berjalan. Token kedaluwarsa: Instagram tidak lagi memberi akses karena token habis dan tidak bisa diperpanjang. Diputuskan: akses dicabut, dari Monomi atau dari pengaturan Instagram.
- EN (25 of max 32 words): What the connection status means. Terhubung (Connected): syncing works. Token kedaluwarsa: Instagram no longer gives access because the token expired and could not be renewed.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Data yang sudah tersinkron tetap tersimpan dan tetap bisa dipakai untuk laporan. Menghubungkan akun Instagram yang berbeda ke klien yang sama hanya bisa setelah akun lama diputuskan dengan Hapus data dicentang. / Data already synced stays and can still be used for reports. Connecting a different Instagram account to the same client only works after the old one is disconnected with Hapus data ticked.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Isi laporan bulanan dari Instagram

- Window: 00:41.6 - 00:54.4 (12.8 s)
- ID (28 of max 29 words): Isi laporan bulanan dari Instagram. Di laporan bulanan klien, buka Tambah bagian data lalu pilih Ambil dari Instagram. Tab ini hanya muncul bila klien punya data Instagram tersinkron.
- EN (31 of max 32 words): Fill the monthly report from Instagram. In the client's monthly report open Tambah bagian data and choose Ambil dari Instagram. The tab only appears when the client has synced Instagram data.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Angka utama yang datanya kurang dari 80% hari dalam sebulan disembunyikan, kecuali Anda mencentang Sertakan metrik dengan data tidak lengkap. Menambah ulang bagian yang sudah ada menggantinya setelah konfirmasi. / Headline numbers based on less than 80% of the month's days are hidden unless you tick Sertakan metrik dengan data tidak lengkap. Adding a section the report already has replaces it after a confirmation.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Putuskan koneksi

- Window: 00:54.4 - 01:04.1 (9.7 s)
- ID (21 of max 22 words): Putuskan koneksi. Klik Putuskan pada kartu. Akses Monomi dihapus dan sinkronisasi berhenti, tetapi data yang sudah tersinkron tetap untuk laporan sebelumnya.
- EN (21 of max 24 words): Disconnect. Click Putuskan on the card. Monomi's access is removed and syncing stops, but already synced data stays for earlier reports.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Klien juga bisa memutuskan sendiri dari portal atau dari pengaturan Instagram. / The client can also disconnect on their own, from the portal or from the Instagram settings.
