- EN: Manage invoices from draft to paid: send, check status, print the PDF, and follow the stamp-duty reminder for invoices above Rp 5.000.000.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Pahami status invoice

- Window: 00:09.3 - 00:20.6 (11.3 s)
- ID (28 of max 26 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Pahami status invoice. Filter status menunjukkan lima status: Draf (belum dikirim), Terkirim (menunggu pembayaran), Lunas (sudah dibayar penuh), Jatuh Tempo (lewat tanggal tempo dan belum lunas), dan Dibatalkan.
- EN (31 of max 28 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Understand invoice statuses. The status filter shows five statuses: Draf (not sent), Terkirim (waiting for payment), Lunas (paid in full), Jatuh Tempo (past the due date and not paid) and Dibatalkan.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Status Lunas dan Jatuh Tempo dihitung dari pembayaran dan tanggal jatuh tempo, jadi Anda cukup mencatat pembayaran. / Lunas and Jatuh Tempo follow from payments and the due date, so you only need to record payments.

- EN (30 of max 31 words): An invoice from a quotation. An invoice created from an approved quotation has status Draf and shows its quotation number. Check the contents, then click Kirim when ready to bill.

- Window: 00:33.1 - 00:42.3 (9.2 s)

- EN (23 of max 22 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): The stamp-duty reminder. 000 stamp on the printed copy before sending it to the client. The line in the summary reads Belum diterapkan.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Penempelan materai dilakukan manual di kertas. Aplikasi hanya mengingatkan dan mencatat bahwa materai sudah ditempel. / Sticking the stamp is a manual step on paper. The app only reminds you and records that the stamp was applied.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Catat bahwa materai sudah ditempel

- Window: 00:42.3 - 00:54.7 (12.5 s)
- ID (25 of max 28 words): Catat bahwa materai sudah ditempel. Setelah materai ditempel, buka Edit pada invoice, centang Materai sudah ditempel, lalu klik Simpan. Peringatan di halaman invoice akan hilang.
- EN (30 of max 31 words): Record that the stamp is applied. After sticking the stamp, open Edit on the invoice, tick Materai sudah ditempel, then click Simpan. The warning on the invoice page goes away.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Cetak atau unduh PDF

- Window: 00:54.7 - 01:06.5 (11.7 s)
- ID (26 of max 27 words): Cetak atau unduh PDF. Di menu Tindakan lainnya pilih Pratinjau PDF. Dokumen terbuka di jendela pratinjau dengan tombol Unduh. Cetak dari sini bila materai harus ditempel.
- EN (24 of max 29 words): Print or download the PDF. In the Tindakan lainnya menu choose Pratinjau PDF. The document opens in a preview window with a Unduh button.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Cetak dulu, tempel materai, baru kirim ke klien bila nilai invoice di atas Rp 5.000.000. / Print first, stick the stamp, and only then send it when the invoice is above Rp 5.000.000.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Kirim invoice

- Window: 01:06.5 - 01:18.6 (12.1 s)
- ID (22 of max 28 words): Kirim invoice. Klik Kirim pada invoice Draf. Status menjadi Terkirim dan tombol Catat Pembayaran muncul. Pembayaran dicatat dengan panduan Pembayaran dan piutang.
- EN (29 of max 30 words): Send the invoice. Click Kirim on a Draf (draft) invoice. The status becomes Terkirim and the Catat Pembayaran button appears. Payments are recorded with the Payments and receivables guide.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Invoice yang sudah Terkirim tidak bisa kembali ke Draf; bila ada salah, edit atau batalkan lalu buat ulang. / A sent invoice cannot return to Draf; if something is wrong, edit or cancel it and create a new one.
