# Narasi / Narration: Publikasi otomatis ke Instagram dan Facebook

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Biarkan Monomi memposting konten milik sendiri ke akun Instagram dan Halaman Facebook pada tanggal dan jam yang direncanakan, lihat hasilnya per platform, dan coba lagi jika gagal.
- EN: Let Monomi post your own content to the Instagram account and Facebook Page on the planned date and time, see the result per platform, and retry when something fails.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Nyalakan Terbitkan otomatis

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:38.9 (38.9 s)
- ID (24 of max 92 words): Nyalakan Terbitkan otomatis. Buka Marketing > Kalender Konten > Monomi. Saat menambah atau mengubah konten, nyalakan Terbitkan otomatis lalu centang Instagram dan/atau Halaman Facebook.
- EN (28 of max 100 words): Turn on Publish automatically. Open Marketing > Content Calendar > Monomi. When you add or edit a post, switch on Publish automatically and tick Instagram and/or Facebook Page.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Hanya admin yang bisa menyalakannya, dan hanya untuk konten milik Monomi. TikTok tetap manual. / Only admins can switch it on, and only for Monomi's own posts. TikTok stays manual.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Syarat media

- Window: 00:38.9 - 00:48.3 (9.3 s)
- ID (19 of max 21 words): Syarat media. Petunjuk di kotak menunjukkan apa yang masih kurang: foto atau video untuk Instagram, serta tanggal dan jam.
- EN (32 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Media requirements. The hints in the box tell you what is still missing: a photo or video for Instagram, and a date and time. 91:1; reels and stories are vertical 9:16 video.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Tambahkan media (2) lebih dulu; petunjuk hilang saat semuanya sudah sesuai. / Add the media (2) first; the hints disappear when everything is in order.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Pantau status per platform

- Window: 00:48.3 - 01:00.4 (12.2 s)
- ID (27 of max 28 words): Pantau status per platform. Di tampilan Daftar setiap platform punya chip sendiri. Biru Terjadwal berarti menunggu waktunya, hijau Terbit berarti sudah tayang, dan merah Gagal perlu ditangani.
- EN (15 of max 30 words): Follow the status per platform. In the List view every platform has its own chip.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Klik chip hijau untuk membuka postingan aslinya. / Click a green chip to open the real post.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Konten yang sudah terbit dan tautannya

- Window: 01:00.4 - 01:12.5 (12.1 s)
- ID (29 of max 28 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Konten yang sudah terbit dan tautannya. Buka konten yang sudah terbit. Panel Publikasi otomatis menunjukkan kapan tiap platform tayang, dan Lihat postingan membuka postingan aslinya di Instagram atau Facebook.
- EN (28 of max 30 words): Published posts and links. Open a published post. The Auto-publishing panel shows when each platform went live, and View post opens the real post on Instagram or Facebook.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Jika gagal: alasan dan Coba lagi

- Window: 01:12.5 - 01:25.9 (13.4 s)
- ID (30 of max 31 words): Jika gagal: alasan dan Coba lagi. Platform yang gagal menampilkan alasannya dengan bahasa yang jelas, misalnya Meta menolak rasio gambar. Perbaiki media atau caption lewat Edit, lalu tekan Coba lagi.
- EN (24 of max 33 words): When it fails: the reason and Retry. A failed platform shows the reason in plain words, for example that Meta rejected the image ratio.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Jika pesannya menyebut koneksi ke Meta terputus, periksa dulu akun atau Halamannya. Tekan Coba lagi hanya jika postingan belum ada, kalau tidak postingan terbit dua kali. / If the message says the connection to Meta dropped, first check the account or Page. Press Retry only if the post is not there, otherwise it is posted twice.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Terbitkan sekarang

- Window: 01:25.9 - 01:35.3 (9.4 s)
- ID (17 of max 21 words): Terbitkan sekarang. Untuk konten terjadwal, panel menawarkan Terbitkan sekarang: konten langsung terbit tanpa menunggu waktu yang direncanakan.
- EN (22 of max 23 words): Publish now. For a scheduled post the panel offers Publish now: it publishes right away instead of waiting for the planned time.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Untuk konten yang tidak diatur terbit otomatis, Terbitkan tetap hanya menandainya sebagai terbit. / For a post that is not set to publish automatically, Publish still just marks it as published.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Periksa koneksi

- Window: 01:35.3 - 01:47.6 (12.3 s)
- ID (27 of max 28 words): Periksa koneksi. Kartu di bagian atas menunjukkan apakah koneksi Meta siap. Klik Periksa koneksi untuk melihat system user, Halaman Facebook, akun Instagram, dan kuota publikasi 24 jam.
- EN (15 of max 31 words): Check the connection. The card at the top says whether the Meta connection is ready.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Jika kartu menyebut belum terhubung, minta developer/admin menghubungkan akun Meta. Jangan kirim token lewat chat. / If the card says it is not connected yet, ask the developer/admin to connect the Meta account. Do not send tokens in chat.
