# Narasi / Narration: Pengaturan CRM: target respons dan tahap pipeline

Video: `crm-pengaturan.67fb4396.mp4` (1280x888, 01:51.2, silent). Guide slug: `crm-pengaturan`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Untuk admin: atur target waktu respons, ganti nama, warna, urutan, dan status tahap pipeline, serta pahami tahap mana yang mengirim event Meta (Dikualifikasi mengirim QualifiedLead, Menang mengirim Purchase).
- EN: For admins: set the response-time target, rename, recolour, reorder and switch pipeline stages, and understand which stage sends which Meta event (Qualified sends QualifiedLead, Won sends Purchase).

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka Pengaturan CRM

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.2 (9.2 s)
- ID (22 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buka Pengaturan CRM. Di menu CRM pilih Pengaturan CRM (halaman khusus admin). Ada dua bagian pertama: Target waktu respons dan Tahap pipeline.
- EN (30 of max 22 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Open CRM settings. In the CRM menu choose Pengaturan CRM (CRM settings, an admin-only page). The first two sections are Target waktu respons (response-time target) and Tahap pipeline (pipeline stages).
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Semua perubahan tahap tersimpan seketika saat Anda mengubahnya; hanya target waktu respons yang memakai tombol Simpan. / Every stage change is saved the moment you make it; only the response-time target uses a Simpan (Save) button.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Atur target waktu respons

- Window: 00:09.2 - 00:20.1 (10.9 s)
- ID (20 of max 25 words): Atur target waktu respons. Isi Menit dengan batas balasan pertama, antara 1 dan 1440 menit (bawaannya 15), lalu klik Simpan.
- EN (26 of max 27 words): Set the response-time target. Fill Menit (minutes) with the limit for a first reply, between 1 and 1440 minutes (the default is 15), then click Simpan.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Pilih angka yang benar-benar bisa dipenuhi tim Anda; target yang terlalu ketat membuat hampir semua lead tampak terlambat. / Choose a number your team can really meet; a target that is too tight makes almost every lead look late.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Baca satu baris tahap

- Window: 00:20.1 - 00:31.8 (11.7 s)
- ID (24 of max 27 words): Baca satu baris tahap. Tiap tahap punya Warna, Nama tahap, Tipe Terbuka, Menang, atau Kalah, Event Meta, saklar Aktif, dan tombol panah untuk mengurutkan.
- EN (35 of max 29 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Read one stage row. Each stage has a Warna (colour), Nama tahap (stage name), Tipe (type) Terbuka (Open), Menang (Won) or Kalah (Lost), an Event Meta, an Aktif (active) switch and arrow buttons to reorder.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Harus selalu ada minimal satu tahap aktif bertipe Terbuka, satu Menang, dan satu Kalah. / There must always be at least one active Open, one Won and one Lost stage.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Ganti nama dan warna

- Window: 00:31.8 - 00:44.6 (12.8 s)
- ID (27 of max 29 words): Ganti nama dan warna. Klik kotak Warna lalu pilih warna baru; warna langsung tersimpan. Ubah Nama tahap lalu tekan Enter atau klik di luar kolom untuk menyimpan.
- EN (31 of max 32 words): Rename and recolour. Click the Warna (colour) box and pick a new colour; it saves at once. Change the Nama tahap and press Enter or click outside the field to save.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Mengganti nama atau warna tidak mengubah tipe dan event Meta tahap itu. / Renaming or recolouring does not change that stage's type or Meta event.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Ubah urutan tahap

- Window: 00:44.6 - 00:57.1 (12.5 s)
- ID (30 of max 29 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Ubah urutan tahap. Gunakan panah Naikkan dan Turunkan pada baris tahap. Urutan ini menentukan susunan kolom di papan lead, dan menentukan mana yang dianggap setelah Dikualifikasi (lihat langkah Event Meta).
- EN (17 of max 31 words): Change the order. Use the Naikkan (move up) and Turunkan (move down) arrows on the stage row.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Tahap pertama tidak bisa dinaikkan dan tahap terakhir tidak bisa diturunkan lagi. / The first stage cannot move up and the last cannot move down.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Nonaktifkan tahap

- Window: 00:57.1 - 01:08.1 (10.9 s)
- ID (24 of max 25 words): Nonaktifkan tahap. Matikan saklar Aktif untuk menyembunyikan sebuah tahap tanpa menghapusnya: barisnya redup dan lead tidak bisa dipindahkan ke sana. Nyalakan lagi kapan saja.
- EN (26 of max 27 words): Switch a stage off. Turn off the Aktif (active) switch to hide a stage without deleting it: its row dims and leads cannot be moved there.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Bila tahap yang membawa event QualifiedLead dinonaktifkan, aturan lompat-melewati-Qualified pada langkah Event Meta tidak berlaku sampai ada tahap aktif lain dengan event itu. / If the stage that carries the QualifiedLead event is switched off, the skip-past-Qualified rule in the Meta event step does not apply until another active stage has that event.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Tambah atau hapus tahap sendiri

- Window: 01:08.1 - 01:21.2 (13.2 s)
- ID (14 of max 30 words): Tambah atau hapus tahap sendiri. Ketik nama di Tahap baru lalu klik Tambah tahap.
- EN (20 of max 33 words): Add or delete your own stage. Type a name in Tahap baru (new stage) and click Tambah tahap (add stage).
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Tahap yang masih berisi lead tidak bisa dihapus, dan tahap yang sudah punya riwayat lead hanya dinonaktifkan karena riwayat tidak dihapus. / A stage that still holds leads cannot be deleted, and a stage that already has lead history is only switched off because history is never deleted.

## Langkah 8 / Step 8: Tahap mana mengirim event Meta apa

- Window: 01:21.2 - 01:29.8 (8.6 s)
- ID (17 of max 19 words): Tahap mana mengirim event Meta apa. Event Meta dicatat saat lead masuk tahap itu, sekali per lead.
- EN (20 of max 21 words): Which stage sends which Meta event. A Meta event is recorded when a lead enters that stage, once per lead.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Lead dari WhatsApp otomatis mengirim LeadSubmitted saat dibuat, jadi opsi itu biasanya tidak perlu dipasang di tahap. Event yang masih antre bisa dikirim lewat tombol Kirim event yang antre pada kartu WhatsApp Business. / Leads from WhatsApp send LeadSubmitted automatically when they are created, so that option normally does not need to be put on a stage. Events still in the queue can be sent with the Kirim event yang antre button on the WhatsApp Business card.

## Langkah 9 / Step 9: Kartu lain di halaman ini

- Window: 01:29.8 - 01:40.5 (10.7 s)
- ID (27 of max 24 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Kartu lain di halaman ini. Pelacakan landing page memantau kunjungan dan ketukan WhatsApp dari landing page iklan, dan Sinkronisasi Meta Ads menarik biaya iklan ke kampanye CRM.
- EN (30 of max 26 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): The other cards on this page. Pelacakan landing page tracks visits and WhatsApp taps from the ad landing page, and Sinkronisasi Meta Ads pulls ad spend into the CRM campaigns.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Nilai-nilai dalam kartu ini diatur di server oleh developer atau admin, bukan diketik di Monomi. / The values in these cards are set on the server by the developer or an admin, not typed into Monomi.

## Langkah 10 / Step 10: Kartu WhatsApp Business

- Window: 01:40.5 - 01:51.2 (10.7 s)
- ID (29 of max 24 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Kartu WhatsApp Business. Kartu WhatsApp Business menampilkan status koneksi WhatsApp, webhook, dan Conversions API. Isinya, termasuk aturan keselamatan untuk nomor WhatsApp iklan, dibahas lengkap di panduan Koneksi WhatsApp (admin).
- EN (19 of max 26 words): The WhatsApp Business card. The WhatsApp Business card shows the WhatsApp connection status, the webhook and the Conversions API.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Baca langkah pertama panduan itu sebelum menyentuh apa pun yang berkaitan dengan nomor WhatsApp. / Read the first step of that guide before touching anything that involves the WhatsApp number.
