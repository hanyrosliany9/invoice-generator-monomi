# Narasi / Narration: Laporan media sosial bulanan

Video: `laporan-bulanan.44bd4f86.mp4` (1280x900, 02:55.3, silent). Guide slug: `laporan-bulanan`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Buat laporan bulanan untuk klien, isi angkanya, lalu kirim ke portal klien.
- EN: Create the monthly report for a client, fill in the numbers and send it to the client portal.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka Laporan Sosial Media dan klik Laporan Baru

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.3 (9.3 s)
- ID (21 of max 21 words): Buka Laporan Sosial Media dan klik Laporan Baru. Halaman ini menampilkan semua laporan dan proyek yang belum punya laporan bulan ini.
- EN (22 of max 23 words): Open Social Media Reports and click New Report. This page lists every report and the projects that still need one this month.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Bulan lalu sudah ada laporan? Pakai Salin laporan terakhir supaya bagian dan grafiknya langsung siap. / Last month already has a report? Use Copy last report so sections and charts are ready.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Pilih proyek dan bulan, lalu klik Simpan & Lanjutkan

- Window: 00:09.3 - 00:24.8 (15.5 s)
- ID (23 of max 36 words): Pilih proyek dan bulan, lalu klik Simpan dan Lanjutkan. Isi proyek, bulan, dan judul. Satu proyek hanya bisa punya satu laporan per bulan.
- EN (26 of max 39 words): Pick the project and month, then click Save and Continue. Choose the project, the month and a title. A project can have one report per month.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Judul terisi otomatis; ubah bila perlu. / The title is filled in for you; change it if needed.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Pilih cara mengisi data

- Window: 00:24.8 - 00:37.3 (12.5 s)
- ID (23 of max 29 words): Pilih cara mengisi data. Satu bagian adalah satu tabel angka beserta grafiknya. Ada tiga cara: unggah file, ketik di tabel, atau angka utama.
- EN (31 of max 31 words): Choose how to fill in the data. A section is one table of numbers with its charts. There are three ways: upload a file, type in a table, or headline numbers.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Cara 1: unggah file CSV atau Excel

- Window: 00:37.3 - 00:47.0 (9.8 s)
- ID (20 of max 22 words): Cara 1: unggah file CSV atau Excel. xls (maks. 5 MB). Belum punya file? Unduh template dengan kolom yang benar.
- EN (23 of max 24 words): Way 1: upload a CSV or Excel file. xls file (max 5 MB). No file yet? Download a template with the right columns.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Satu baris per hari. Kolom tanggal dikenali otomatis. / One row per day. The date column is recognised automatically.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Periksa hasil bacaan file

- Window: 00:47.0 - 00:57.8 (10.8 s)
- ID (21 of max 24 words): Periksa hasil bacaan file. Aplikasi menampilkan jumlah baris dan kolom, jenis tiap kolom, dan beberapa baris pertama. Periksa sebelum menambah bagian.
- EN (30 of max 27 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Check how the file was read. The app shows the number of rows and columns, the type of each column and the first rows. Check them before adding the section.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Jika ada tanggal di luar bulan laporan, Anda akan diminta mengonfirmasi dulu. / If some dates fall outside the report month you will be asked to confirm.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Cara 2: ketik langsung di tabel

- Window: 00:57.8 - 01:21.4 (23.6 s)
- ID (23 of max 55 words): Cara 2: ketik langsung di tabel. Pilih tabel awal seperti Instagram harian; semua tanggal bulan itu sudah terisi. Lalu ketik angkanya di sel.
- EN (30 of max 60 words): Way 2: type straight into the table. Pick a starting table such as Instagram daily; every date of the month is already there. Then type the numbers into the cells.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Angka boleh ditulis 1.234 atau 12,5. / Numbers can be written 1.234 or 12,5.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Atau tempel dari Excel

- Window: 01:21.4 - 01:32.7 (11.3 s)
- ID (19 of max 26 words): Atau tempel dari Excel. Klik Tempel dari Excel, tempel tabel beserta baris judulnya, lalu klik Pakai tabel yang ditempel.
- EN (20 of max 28 words): Or paste from Excel. Click Paste from Excel, paste the table including the header row, then click Use pasted table.

## Langkah 8 / Step 8: Cara 3: isi angka utama saja

- Window: 01:32.7 - 01:45.5 (12.8 s)
- ID (25 of max 29 words): Cara 3: isi angka utama saja. Cocok bila hanya ada beberapa total, misalnya pengikut baru atau engagement rate. Tiap angka menjadi satu kartu di laporan.
- EN (31 of max 32 words): Way 3: just fill in the headline numbers. Good when there are only a few totals, such as new followers or engagement rate. Each number becomes a card in the report.

## Langkah 9 / Step 9: Atur grafik

- Window: 01:45.5 - 01:55.3 (9.8 s)
- ID (17 of max 22 words): Atur grafik. Setelah bagian dibuat, grafik muncul otomatis. Ubah tipe grafik, kolom yang ditampilkan, lalu klik Simpan.
- EN (21 of max 24 words): Set up the charts. Charts appear automatically once the section exists. Change the chart type, the columns shown, then click Save.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Grafik tanpa data disembunyikan dari klien. / Charts without data are hidden from the client.

## Langkah 10 / Step 10: Pratinjau seperti yang dilihat klien

- Window: 01:55.3 - 02:06.6 (11.3 s)
- ID (21 of max 26 words): Pratinjau seperti yang dilihat klien. Klik Pratinjau sebagai klien. Tampilannya sama persis dengan portal; klien belum bisa melihatnya selama masih draf.
- EN (27 of max 28 words): Preview what the client will see. Click Preview as client. It looks exactly like the portal; the client cannot see it while it is still a draft.

## Langkah 11 / Step 11: Tandai selesai: tayangkan di portal

- Window: 02:06.6 - 02:19.6 (13.0 s)
- ID (29 of max 30 words): Tandai selesai: tayangkan di portal. Di halaman laporan, buka menu Tindakan lainnya dan pilih Tayangkan di portal (tanpa email). Status berubah menjadi Selesai dan laporan langsung terlihat oleh klien.
- EN (23 of max 32 words): Mark as done: publish to the portal. On the report page open the More actions menu and choose Publish to portal (no email).

## Langkah 12 / Step 12: Kirim ke klien

- Window: 02:19.6 - 02:30.9 (11.3 s)
- ID (25 of max 26 words): Kirim ke klien. Klik Kirim ke klien untuk mengirim email berisi tautan ke semua kontak portal yang aktif. Periksa daftar penerima, lalu klik Kirim email.
- EN (24 of max 28 words): Send to client. Click Send to client to email a link to every active portal contact. Check the recipient list, then click Send email.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Belum ada kontak? Tambahkan di kartu Portal Klien pada halaman klien. / No contacts yet? Add them in the Client Portal card on the client page.

## Langkah 13 / Step 13: Kembalikan ke draf bila perlu mengubah

- Window: 02:30.9 - 02:41.5 (10.6 s)
- ID (26 of max 24 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Kembalikan ke draf bila perlu mengubah. Laporan yang sudah tayang bisa dikembalikan ke draf lewat Kembalikan ke draf. Laporan hilang dari portal sampai Anda menayangkannya lagi.
- EN (31 of max 26 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Move back to draft when you need to edit. A live report can go back to draft with Back to draft. It disappears from the portal until you publish it again.

## Langkah 14 / Step 14: Salin ke bulan lain

- Window: 02:41.5 - 02:55.3 (13.8 s)
- ID (31 of max 32 words): Salin ke bulan lain. Buka Tindakan lainnya, pilih Salin ke bulan depan, tentukan bulan dan tahun, lalu klik Salin. Bagian dan grafik ikut tersalin sebagai draf; Anda hanya mengisi angka baru.
- EN (20 of max 34 words): Copy to another month. Open More actions, choose Copy to next month, pick the month and year, then click Copy.
