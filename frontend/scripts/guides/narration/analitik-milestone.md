# Narasi / Narration: Analitik milestone pembayaran

Video: `analitik-milestone.9523dc78.mp4` (1280x1066, 00:25.2, silent). Guide slug: `analitik-milestone`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Baca halaman Milestone: seberapa cepat dan tepat klien membayar tiap termin (misalnya DP 30% dan Pelunasan 70%), pendapatan yang sudah diakui, dan proyeksi arus kas.
- EN: Read the Milestone page: how fast and how punctually clients pay each stage (for example 30% down payment and 70% final payment), the revenue already recognised, and the cash flow forecast.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka Milestone dan baca ringkasannya

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:05.2 (5.2 s)
- ID (9 of max 11 words): Buka Milestone dan baca ringkasannya. Klik Milestone di menu.
- EN (11 of max 12 words): Open Milestone and read the summary. Click Milestone in the menu.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Rentang waktu memakai tanggal milestone dibuat. Muat Ulang memperbarui angka setelah ada pembayaran baru. / The range uses the date the milestone was created. Muat Ulang (refresh) updates the figures after a new payment.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Siklus pembayaran dan profitabilitas

- Window: 00:05.2 - 00:14.3 (9.1 s)
- ID (32 of max 20 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Siklus pembayaran dan profitabilitas. Tiga kartu: Siklus Pembayaran (rata-rata hari dari tagihan sampai dibayar), Tepat Waktu (persentase milestone yang dibayar sebelum jatuh tempo), dan Pengakuan Pendapatan (persentase nilai milestone yang sudah diakui).
- EN (35 of max 22 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Payment cycle and profitability. Three cards: Siklus Pembayaran (average days from billing to payment), Tepat Waktu (the percentage of milestones paid by their due date) and Pengakuan Pendapatan (the percentage of milestone value already recognised).
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Siklus yang makin panjang berarti klien makin lambat membayar; tagih lebih awal atau ubah porsi DP. / A growing cycle means clients pay more slowly; bill earlier or change the down payment share.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Detail tiap milestone

- Window: 00:14.3 - 00:25.2 (10.9 s)
- ID (29 of max 25 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Detail tiap milestone. Tabel Detail Milestone mendaftar tiap termin: nomor, nama, Jatuh Tempo, Jumlah, nilai yang Diakui, Hari Bayar, dan Status. Jumlah yang jatuh tempo ditandai di judul tabel.
- EN (30 of max 27 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Detail of each milestone. The Detail Milestone table lists each stage: number, name, Jatuh Tempo (due date), Jumlah (amount), the Diakui (recognised) value, Hari Bayar (days to pay) and Status.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Cara memilih termin pada quotation ada di panduan Quotation, langkah Atur ketentuan pembayaran. / How to choose stages on a quotation is in the Quotation guide, in the step Atur ketentuan pembayaran (set the payment terms).
