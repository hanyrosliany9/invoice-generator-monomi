# Narasi / Narration: Klien: tambah, ubah, dan lihat riwayat

Video: `klien-baru.ad8c6f9d.mp4` (1280x820, 01:29.6, silent). Guide slug: `klien-baru`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Catat klien baru lengkap dengan kontak, NPWP, dan rekening, lalu lihat semua proyek, penawaran, dan invoice-nya di satu halaman.
- EN: Record a new client with contact, tax number and bank details, then see all their projects, quotations and invoices on one page.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka menu Klien

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.3 (9.3 s)
- ID (25 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buka menu Klien. Daftar ini memuat semua klien. Klik Klien Baru untuk menambah, atau ketik nama di kolom pencarian untuk mencari klien yang sudah ada.
- EN (28 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Open the Klien menu. This list holds every client. Click Klien Baru to add one, or type a name in the search box to find an existing client.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Cari dulu sebelum menambah, supaya klien yang sama tidak tercatat dua kali. / Search first, so the same client is not recorded twice.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Isi identitas klien

- Window: 00:09.3 - 00:26.4 (17.1 s)
- ID (19 of max 40 words): Isi identitas klien. Isi Nama Klien dan Perusahaan. Isi NPWP bila klien memilikinya, karena NPWP ikut tercetak di dokumen.
- EN (24 of max 43 words): Fill in the client identity. Enter the Nama Klien and Perusahaan. Add the NPWP if the client has one; it is printed on documents.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Hanya Nama Klien yang wajib. Data lain bisa dilengkapi nanti lewat tombol Edit. Status Klien Aktif dibiarkan menyala. / Only Nama Klien is required. You can complete the rest later with Edit. Leave the Status Klien switch on (Aktif).

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Isi kontak, rekening, dan alamat

- Window: 00:26.4 - 00:46.8 (20.4 s)
- ID (31 of max 48 words): Isi kontak, rekening, dan alamat. Isi Narahubung, Email, dan Telepon. Rekening Bank ditulis dengan format bank, nomor rekening, dan nama pemilik. Alamat Lengkap dipakai di dokumen resmi seperti invoice dan kwitansi.
- EN (24 of max 52 words): Add contact, bank account and address. Fill in the Narahubung, Email and Telepon. Write the Rekening Bank as bank, account number and owner name.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Email kontak juga dipakai saat Anda memberi akses ke portal klien (lihat panduan Portal klien). / The contact e-mail is also used when you give the client portal access (see the client portal guide).

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Halaman klien setelah disimpan

- Window: 00:46.8 - 00:58.5 (11.7 s)
- ID (26 of max 27 words): Halaman klien setelah disimpan. Setelah disimpan, Anda langsung dibawa ke halaman klien. Dari sini klik Edit untuk mengubah data, atau menu tiga titik untuk aksi lain.
- EN (28 of max 29 words): The client page after saving. After saving you land on the client page. From here click Edit to change the data, or the three-dot menu for other actions.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Lihat riwayat proyek

- Window: 00:58.5 - 01:07.9 (9.4 s)
- ID (19 of max 21 words): Lihat riwayat proyek. Gulir ke bawah: kartu Riwayat Proyek menampilkan semua proyek klien ini. Klik satu proyek untuk membukanya.
- EN (22 of max 23 words): View the project history. Scroll down: the Riwayat Proyek card lists all projects of this client. Click a project to open it.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Lihat riwayat penawaran dan invoice

- Window: 01:07.9 - 01:17.9 (10.1 s)
- ID (28 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Lihat riwayat penawaran dan invoice. Riwayat Invoice dan Riwayat Penawaran menunjukkan seluruh dokumen penagihan klien beserta statusnya, jadi Anda tahu mana yang belum dibayar tanpa membuka menu lain.
- EN (32 of max 25 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): View quotation and invoice history. Riwayat Invoice and Riwayat Penawaran show every billing document of the client with its status, so you can see what is still unpaid without opening other menus.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Ubah data klien

- Window: 01:17.9 - 01:29.6 (11.7 s)
- ID (24 of max 27 words): Ubah data klien. Klik Edit di halaman klien, ubah kolom yang perlu, lalu klik Simpan. Perubahan berlaku untuk dokumen baru; dokumen lama tidak berubah.
- EN (28 of max 29 words): Edit client data. Click Edit on the client page, change the fields you need, then click Simpan. Changes apply to new documents; existing documents stay as they are.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Klien yang sudah tidak aktif sebaiknya dimatikan lewat Status Klien (Aktif), bukan dihapus, agar riwayatnya tetap ada. / A client you no longer work with is better switched off with Status Klien (Aktif) than deleted, so the history is kept.
