# Narasi / Narration: Quotation: dari penawaran ke invoice

Video: `quotation.e9abcb9b.mp4` (1280x820, 01:51.6, silent). Guide slug: `quotation`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Buat penawaran dengan item dan PPN, kirim ke klien, catat persetujuan atau penolakan, buat revisi, dan lihat invoice yang terbentuk otomatis.
- EN: Create a quotation with items and VAT, send it to the client, record approval or rejection, make a revision, and see the invoice that is created automatically.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka menu Quotation

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.3 (9.3 s)
- ID (22 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buka menu Quotation. Daftar penawaran beserta statusnya: Draf, Terkirim, Disetujui, Ditolak. Klik Penawaran Baru untuk membuat, dan gunakan filter status untuk menyaring.
- EN (30 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Open the Quotation menu. The list of quotations with their status: Draf, Terkirim, Disetujui, Ditolak. Click Penawaran Baru to create one, and use the status filter to narrow the list.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Alur penawaran: Draf, Terkirim, lalu Disetujui atau Ditolak. / Quotation flow: Draf, Terkirim, then Disetujui or Ditolak.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Pilih klien dan proyek

- Window: 00:09.3 - 00:22.0 (12.6 s)
- ID (21 of max 29 words): Pilih klien dan proyek. Pilih Klien, lalu Proyek milik klien itu. Atur tanggal Berlaku Sampai: setelah tanggal ini penawaran dianggap kedaluwarsa.
- EN (28 of max 31 words): Choose the client and project. Pick the Klien, then the Proyek that belongs to that client. Set the Berlaku Sampai date: after it the quotation counts as expired.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Proyek yang sudah punya daftar produk akan mengisi item penawaran secara otomatis. / A project that already has a product list fills in the quotation items automatically.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Isi item penawaran

- Window: 00:22.0 - 00:43.8 (21.9 s)
- ID (19 of max 51 words): Isi item penawaran. Tulis nama item, deskripsi, jumlah, dan harga. Klik Tambah item untuk baris berikutnya. Subtotal dihitung otomatis.
- EN (25 of max 55 words): Fill in the quotation items. Write the item name, description, quantity and price. Click Tambah item for the next row. The subtotal is calculated automatically.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Aktifkan PPN bila perlu

- Window: 00:43.8 - 00:53.9 (10.0 s)
- ID (27 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Aktifkan PPN bila perlu. Centang Sertakan PPN 11% bila klien memerlukannya, biasanya untuk klien ber-NPWP. Ringkasan Nilai menampilkan subtotal, pajak, dan total yang akan tertera di dokumen.
- EN (19 of max 25 words): Turn on VAT when needed. Tick Sertakan PPN 11% when the client needs it, usually clients with an NPWP.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Pastikan harga di item belum termasuk PPN; aplikasi menambahkan 11% di atas subtotal. / Make sure the item prices do not include VAT yet; the app adds 11% on top of the subtotal.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Atur ketentuan pembayaran

- Window: 00:53.9 - 01:05.6 (11.7 s)
- ID (26 of max 27 words): Atur ketentuan pembayaran. Pilih Pembayaran Penuh atau Termin bila pembayaran dicicil, lalu periksa Syarat and Ketentuan yang tercetak di dokumen. Klik Simpan; penawaran tersimpan sebagai Draf.
- EN (24 of max 29 words): Set the payment terms. Choose Pembayaran Penuh or Termin if payment is in instalments, then check the Syarat and Ketentuan printed on the document.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Penawaran Draf masih bisa diubah lewat menu Aksi penawaran, pilihan Ubah. / A draft can still be changed from the Aksi penawaran menu, option Ubah.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Kirim penawaran

- Window: 01:05.6 - 01:17.4 (11.7 s)
- ID (26 of max 27 words): Kirim penawaran. Buka penawaran Draf dan klik Kirim. Status berubah menjadi Terkirim. Menu tiga titik, Aksi penawaran, berisi Ubah dan Pratinjau PDF untuk mengecek tampilan dokumen.
- EN (15 of max 29 words): Send the quotation. Open the draft quotation and click Kirim. The status changes to Terkirim.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Gunakan Pratinjau PDF sebelum mengirim untuk memastikan data klien dan harga sudah benar. / Use Pratinjau PDF before sending to be sure client data and prices are right.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Catat keputusan klien

- Window: 01:17.4 - 01:27.5 (10.1 s)
- ID (19 of max 23 words): Catat keputusan klien. Setelah klien menjawab, buka penawaran berstatus Terkirim. Klik Setujui bila klien setuju, atau Tolak bila tidak.
- EN (24 of max 25 words): Record the client's decision. When the client replies, open the quotation with status Terkirim. Click Setujui if the client agrees, or Tolak if not.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Catat keputusan segera setelah klien memberi kabar, supaya invoice bisa dibuat tepat waktu. / Record the decision as soon as the client replies, so the invoice can be created on time.

## Langkah 8 / Step 8: Penawaran disetujui menjadi invoice

- Window: 01:27.5 - 01:39.5 (12.0 s)
- ID (23 of max 27 words): Penawaran disetujui menjadi invoice. Penawaran yang disetujui berstatus Disetujui dan invoice dibuat otomatis berstatus Draf (lihat panduan Invoice). Klik Lihat Invoice untuk membukanya.
- EN (30 of max 30 words): An approved quotation becomes an invoice. An approved quotation shows Disetujui and an invoice is created automatically with status Draft (see the Invoice guide). Click Lihat Invoice to open it.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Salah setujui? Menu Aksi penawaran punya Buka Kembali (Batalkan Persetujuan), yang membatalkan invoice yang belum dibayar. / Approved by mistake? The Aksi penawaran menu has Buka Kembali (Batalkan Persetujuan), which voids the invoices that are not yet paid.

## Langkah 9 / Step 9: Buat revisi dari penawaran yang ditolak

- Window: 01:39.5 - 01:51.6 (12.0 s)
- ID (32 of max 27 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buat revisi dari penawaran yang ditolak. Penawaran berstatus Ditolak bisa direvisi: klik Buat Revisi. Aplikasi membuat salinan Draf baru dan menandai yang lama sebagai Direvisi. Ubah harga atau lingkup, lalu kirim ulang.
- EN (29 of max 30 words): Revise a rejected quotation. A quotation with status Ditolak can be revised: click Buat Revisi. The app creates a new draft copy and marks the old one as Direvisi.
