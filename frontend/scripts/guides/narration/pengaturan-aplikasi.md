# Narasi / Narration: Pengaturan dan aplikasi mobile

Video: `pengaturan-aplikasi.4a1f6747.mp4` (1280x1000, 01:25.2, silent). Guide slug: `pengaturan-aplikasi`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Kenali tiap bagian Pengaturan: profil, kata sandi, identitas perusahaan, rekening, penomoran invoice, notifikasi, cadangan data, dan cara memasang aplikasi Android Monomi di ponsel.
- EN: Learn every part of Settings: profile, password, company identity, bank accounts, invoice numbering, notifications, data backup, and how to install the Monomi Android app on a phone.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka Pengaturan

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.2 (9.2 s)
- ID (31 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buka Pengaturan. Klik Pengaturan di menu. Daftar bagian ada di kiri dan isinya di kanan; setiap bagian disimpan sendiri-sendiri dengan tombolnya sendiri. Semua staf melihat Profil, Keamanan, Notifikasi, dan Aplikasi Mobile.
- EN (8 of max 22 words): Open Settings. Click Pengaturan (Settings) in the menu.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Bila Anda mengetik lalu berpindah bagian tanpa menyimpan, Monomi bertanya dulu apakah perubahan boleh dibuang. / If you type and then switch section without saving, Monomi first asks whether the changes may be discarded.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Profil

- Window: 00:09.2 - 00:18.4 (9.2 s)
- ID (22 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Profil. Ubah Nama Lengkap, Zona Waktu, dan Bahasa tampilan akun Anda, lalu klik Simpan Profil. Tombol Simpan baru aktif setelah ada perubahan.
- EN (30 of max 22 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Profile. Change the Nama Lengkap (full name), Zona Waktu (time zone) and Bahasa (language) of your account, then click Simpan Profil. The save button only becomes active once something changed.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Bahasa juga bisa diganti cepat dengan tombol EN / ID di bilah atas. / The language can also be switched quickly with the EN / ID button in the top bar.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Ubah kata sandi (Keamanan)

- Window: 00:18.4 - 00:31.9 (13.6 s)
- ID (22 of max 31 words): Ubah kata sandi (Keamanan). Isi Kata Sandi Saat Ini, Kata Sandi Baru, dan Konfirmasi Kata Sandi Baru, lalu klik Ubah Kata Sandi.
- EN (28 of max 34 words): Change your password (Keamanan). Fill in Kata Sandi Saat Ini (current password), Kata Sandi Baru (new password) and Konfirmasi Kata Sandi Baru (confirm), then click Ubah Kata Sandi.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Ikon mata di tiap kolom menampilkan atau menyembunyikan isinya. Kata sandi awal pengguna baru diatur admin di Pengguna dan peran; setelah itu pemiliknya mengubahnya sendiri di sini. / The eye icon in each field shows or hides its content. The first password of a new user is set by an admin in Users and roles; after that the owner changes it here.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Perusahaan (khusus admin)

- Window: 00:31.9 - 00:38.7 (6.7 s)
- ID (14 of max 15 words): Perusahaan (khusus admin). XXX, Alamat, Telepon, Email, Website, dan Mata Uang. Klik Simpan Perusahaan.
- EN (14 of max 16 words): Company (admin only). XXX, Alamat (address), phone, email, website and currency. Click Simpan Perusahaan.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Periksa NPWP dan alamat dengan teliti: keduanya tercetak di setiap invoice. / Check the NPWP and address carefully: both are printed on every invoice.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Rekening Bank (khusus admin)

- Window: 00:38.7 - 00:46.5 (7.8 s)
- ID (15 of max 17 words): Rekening Bank (khusus admin). Hingga tiga rekening yang dicetak di footer invoice sebagai tujuan pembayaran.
- EN (18 of max 19 words): Bank accounts (admin only). Up to three bank accounts are printed in the invoice footer as payment destinations.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Rekening ini juga mengisi Informasi Pembayaran secara otomatis pada form Invoice Baru. / These accounts also fill Informasi Pembayaran automatically on the New Invoice form.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Invoice dan penomoran (khusus admin)

- Window: 00:46.5 - 00:56.1 (9.7 s)
- ID (21 of max 22 words): Invoice dan penomoran (khusus admin). Pilih Termin Pembayaran Default: NET 7, 14, 30, atau 60 hari. 000, dan tidak dapat diubah.
- EN (21 of max 24 words): Invoicing and numbering (admin only). Choose the Termin Pembayaran Default (default payment terms): NET 7, 14, 30 or 60 days. 000.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Aturan materai ditetapkan hukum: total lebih dari Rp 5.000.000, bukan sama dengan, pada form invoice dan penawaran. Ganti prefix hanya bila sungguh perlu, karena nomor lama tidak berubah. / The stamp duty rule is fixed by law: a total of more than Rp 5.000.000, not equal to it, on the invoice and quotation forms. Change a prefix only when really needed, because old numbers do not change.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Notifikasi

- Window: 00:56.1 - 01:07.6 (11.5 s)
- ID (31 of max 26 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Notifikasi. Pilih bagaimana sistem menghubungi Anda untuk kejadian penting: Notifikasi Email untuk invoice jatuh tempo, pembayaran masuk, dan ringkasan harian, serta Notifikasi Push untuk peringatan langsung di browser saat Anda online.
- EN (32 of max 28 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Notifications. Choose how the system contacts you about important events: Notifikasi Email for overdue invoices, incoming payments and the daily digest, and Notifikasi Push for instant browser alerts while you are online.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Pengaturan ini milik Anda pribadi dan tidak mengubah notifikasi staf lain. / These settings are personal and do not change other staff members' notifications.

## Langkah 8 / Step 8: Cadangan data (khusus admin)

- Window: 01:07.6 - 01:19.5 (11.9 s)
- ID (23 of max 27 words): Cadangan data (khusus admin). Nyalakan Backup Otomatis, pilih Frekuensi Harian, Mingguan, atau Bulanan, dan Waktu Backup (zona waktu WIB), lalu klik Simpan Cadangan.
- EN (31 of max 29 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Data backup (admin only). Turn on Backup Otomatis (automatic backup), choose the Frekuensi Harian (daily), Mingguan (weekly) or Bulanan (monthly) and the Waktu Backup (WIB time zone), then click Simpan Cadangan.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Berkas .sql berisi seluruh data bisnis. Simpan di tempat yang aman dan jangan dikirim lewat chat atau email biasa. / The .sql file contains all business data. Keep it somewhere safe and never send it through chat or ordinary email.

## Langkah 9 / Step 9: Pasang aplikasi Android

- Window: 01:19.5 - 01:25.2 (5.6 s)
- ID (10 of max 12 words): Pasang aplikasi Android. Klik Unduh APK di ponsel Android Anda.
- EN (11 of max 13 words): Install the Android app. On your Android phone click Unduh APK.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Aplikasi ini berupa berkas APK dan hanya untuk Android. Di iPhone atau komputer, buka Monomi lewat browser. Matikan lagi izin instal dari sumber tidak dikenal setelah selesai. / The app is an APK file and only for Android. On an iPhone or a computer, open Monomi in the browser. Switch the unknown-sources permission off again afterwards.
