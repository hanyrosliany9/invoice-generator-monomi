# Narasi / Narration: Perencana konten

Video: `perencana-konten.4cf26c33.mp4` (1280x900, 02:09.5, silent). Guide slug: `perencana-konten`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Rencanakan, jadwalkan, dan pratinjau konten Instagram dan TikTok untuk satu klien.
- EN: Plan, schedule and preview Instagram and TikTok content for one client.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Pilih klien di Kalender Konten

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:08.6 (8.6 s)
- ID (17 of max 19 words): Pilih klien di Kalender Konten. Buka Kalender Konten lalu pilih klien. Monomi sendiri juga ada, bertanda Internal.
- EN (20 of max 21 words): Pick a client in Content Calendar. Open Content Calendar and choose a client. Monomi itself is there too, marked Internal.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Hanya klien yang punya proyek Manajemen Media Sosial yang muncul. / Only clients with a Social Media Management project appear.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Kenali halaman kalender

- Window: 00:08.6 - 00:18.8 (10.1 s)
- ID (19 of max 23 words): Kenali halaman kalender. Tambah Konten membuat konten baru. Tab di atas kalender mengganti tampilan: Bulan, Daftar, Instagram, dan TikTok.
- EN (24 of max 25 words): Get to know the calendar page. Add Content creates new content. The tabs above the calendar switch the view: Month, List, Instagram and TikTok.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Tulis keterangan, pilih platform dan tipe konten

- Window: 00:18.8 - 00:31.1 (12.4 s)
- ID (18 of max 28 words): Tulis keterangan, pilih platform dan tipe konten. Isi keterangan, pilih platform, lalu tipe: Foto/Video, Carousel, Reel, atau Story.
- EN (23 of max 31 words): Write the caption, pick platforms and content type. Fill in the caption, pick the platforms, then the type: Photo/Video, Carousel, Reel or Story.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Setiap tipe punya aturan media sendiri, tertulis di bawah pilihannya. / Each type has its own media rules, shown under the option.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Tambahkan media dan atur jadwal

- Window: 00:31.1 - 00:43.8 (12.7 s)
- ID (32 of max 29 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Tambahkan media dan atur jadwal. Klik Tambah untuk memilih media, lalu pilih tanggal dan jam. Jam selalu dalam WIB, tidak mengikuti zona waktu browser. Klik Jadwalkan; tanpa tanggal, konten tersimpan sebagai draf.
- EN (26 of max 31 words): Add media and set the schedule. Click Add to choose media, then pick a date and time. Times are always WIB, not your browser time zone.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Buka konten untuk melihat aksinya

- Window: 00:43.8 - 00:56.2 (12.4 s)
- ID (23 of max 28 words): Buka konten untuk melihat aksinya. Klik konten di kalender. Duplikat membuat salinan draf, Terbitkan menandainya sudah tayang. Ada juga Edit, Arsipkan, dan Hapus.
- EN (30 of max 31 words): Open a post to see its actions. Click a post on the calendar. Duplicate makes a draft copy, Publish marks it as live. There are also Edit, Archive and Delete.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Tandai sudah terbit

- Window: 00:56.2 - 01:03.3 (7.1 s)
- ID (14 of max 16 words): Tandai sudah terbit. Tentukan tanggal dan jam tayang yang sebenarnya, lalu klik Tandai terbit.
- EN (16 of max 17 words): Mark as published. Set the real date and time it went live, then click Mark published.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Konten berlabel Terlambat berarti jadwalnya sudah lewat; tandai terbit atau jadwalkan ulang. / A post labelled Overdue has a schedule in the past; mark it published or reschedule it.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Seret konten untuk menjadwal ulang

- Window: 01:03.3 - 01:14.2 (10.9 s)
- ID (20 of max 25 words): Seret konten untuk menjadwal ulang. Seret kartu konten ke tanggal lain di tampilan Bulan. Jam tetap. Klik Urungkan bila salah.
- EN (26 of max 27 words): Drag a post to reschedule. Drag a post card to another day in the Month view. The time stays. Click Undo if it was a mistake.

## Langkah 8 / Step 8: Pilih beberapa konten sekaligus

- Window: 01:14.2 - 01:25.5 (11.3 s)
- ID (22 of max 26 words): Pilih beberapa konten sekaligus. Di tampilan Daftar, centang beberapa konten. Bilah di atas menyediakan Ubah status, Geser jadwal, dan Hapus untuk semuanya.
- EN (25 of max 28 words): Select several posts at once. In List view tick several posts. The bar above offers Change status, Shift schedule and Delete for all of them.

## Langkah 9 / Step 9: Lihat konten seperti di profil Instagram

- Window: 01:25.5 - 01:36.4 (10.9 s)
- ID (22 of max 25 words): Lihat konten seperti di profil Instagram. Tab Instagram menampilkan profil klien dan grid kontennya. Klik Buka pratinjau ponsel untuk tampilan layar penuh.
- EN (26 of max 27 words): See the posts as on an Instagram profile. The Instagram tab shows the client profile and its grid. Click Open phone preview for a full-screen view.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Profil diambil dari data klien; isi username dan bio di halaman klien. / The profile comes from the client record; fill in username and bio on the client page.

## Langkah 10 / Step 10: Kelola Highlight Instagram

- Window: 01:36.4 - 01:46.1 (9.8 s)
- ID (19 of max 22 words): Kelola Highlight Instagram. Klik Highlights, beri judul, pilih media vertikal 9:16, lalu klik Buat Highlight. Media pertama menjadi sampul.
- EN (23 of max 24 words): Manage Instagram Highlights. Click Highlights, give it a title, choose vertical 9:16 media, then click Create Highlight. The first media becomes the cover.

## Langkah 11 / Step 11: Lihat konten seperti di TikTok

- Window: 01:46.1 - 01:56.7 (10.5 s)
- ID (20 of max 24 words): Lihat konten seperti di TikTok. Tab TikTok menampilkan profil TikTok klien. Klik Buka pratinjau TikTok untuk melihat dalam bingkai ponsel.
- EN (25 of max 26 words): See the posts as on TikTok. The TikTok tab shows the client TikTok profile. Click Open TikTok preview to view it in a phone frame.

## Langkah 12 / Step 12: Bagikan pratinjau ke klien lewat tautan

- Window: 01:56.7 - 02:09.5 (12.9 s)
- ID (25 of max 29 words): Bagikan pratinjau ke klien lewat tautan. Klik Bagikan, lalu Aktifkan tautan berbagi. Salin tautannya dan kirim ke klien; mereka tidak perlu login. Nonaktifkan kapan saja.
- EN (31 of max 32 words): Share the preview with the client by link. Click Share, then Enable sharing link. Copy the link and send it to the client; no login needed. Turn it off any time.
