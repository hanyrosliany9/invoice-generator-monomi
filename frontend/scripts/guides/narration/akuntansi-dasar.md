# Narasi / Narration: Akuntansi dasar: jurnal dan laporan

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Cara melihat jurnal, mencatat jurnal manual, membaca saldo kas dan bank, dan membuka laporan laba rugi, neraca, dan bagan akun.
- EN: How to view journals, record a manual journal, read cash and bank balances, and open the profit and loss statement, balance sheet and chart of accounts.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Lihat Jurnal

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.2 (9.2 s)
- ID (20 of max 21 words): Lihat Jurnal. Menu Akuntansi, Jurnal memuat semua jurnal, otomatis maupun manual. Kartu di atas merangkum jumlah entri dan total debit.
- EN (25 of max 22 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): View the Jurnal. Menu Akuntansi, Jurnal holds every journal, automatic and manual. The cards at the top summarise the number of entries and total debit.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Invoice, pembayaran, pengeluaran, dan gaji membuat jurnalnya sendiri. Anda tidak perlu mengisinya lagi. / Invoices, payments, expenses and salaries create their own journals. You do not need to enter them again.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Mulai jurnal manual

- Window: 00:09.2 - 00:22.4 (13.2 s)
- ID (22 of max 30 words): Mulai jurnal manual. Pilih Tipe Transaksi, isi Deskripsi minimal 10 karakter, dan Nomor Dokumen bila ada bukti. Tanggal entry menentukan periode fiskalnya.
- EN (32 of max 33 words): Start a manual journal. Choose the Tipe Transaksi, enter a Deskripsi of at least 10 characters, and a Nomor Dokumen if there is a voucher. The entry date decides the fiscal period.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Gunakan jurnal manual untuk hal yang tidak punya halaman sendiri, misalnya setoran modal atau penyesuaian. / Use manual journals for things that have no page of their own, such as an owner's capital deposit or an adjustment.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Isi baris jurnal

- Window: 00:22.4 - 00:43.4 (21.0 s)
- ID (27 of max 49 words): Isi baris jurnal. Setiap baris memilih akun, lalu debit atau kredit. Total debit harus sama dengan total kredit; kartu Saldo Jurnal berubah menjadi Seimbang bila sudah benar.
- EN (32 of max 53 words): Fill in the journal lines. Each line picks an account, then a debit or credit. Total debit must equal total credit; the Saldo Jurnal card turns to Seimbang when it is right.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Jurnal yang sudah diposting tidak diubah; koreksi dibuat dengan jurnal pembalik atau penyesuaian. / A posted journal is not edited; corrections are made with a reversing or adjusting entry.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Baca Saldo Kas & Bank

- Window: 00:43.4 - 00:58.8 (15.4 s)
- ID (31 of max 36 words): Baca Saldo Kas dan Bank. Pilih periode untuk melihat saldo awal, masuk, keluar, dan saldo akhir tiap akun kas dan bank. Total Kas and Bank dihitung dari jurnal yang sudah diposting.
- EN (24 of max 39 words): Read Saldo Kas and Bank. Choose the period to see the opening balance, inflow, outflow and closing balance of each cash and bank account.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Buka Laporan Laba Rugi

- Window: 00:58.8 - 01:08.9 (10.1 s)
- ID (19 of max 23 words): Buka Laporan Laba Rugi. Pilih rentang tanggal untuk melihat pendapatan dikurangi beban sampai laba bersih. Tombol Ekspor mengunduh laporannya.
- EN (24 of max 25 words): Open the Laporan Laba Rugi. Choose the date range to see revenue minus expenses down to net profit. The Ekspor button downloads the report.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Laporan keuangan lain ada di menu Akuntansi, Laporan: Buku Besar, Arus Kas, Neraca, dan Neraca Saldo. / The other financial reports are in menu Akuntansi, Laporan: Buku Besar, Arus Kas, Neraca and Neraca Saldo.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Buka Neraca

- Window: 01:08.9 - 01:21.1 (12.2 s)
- ID (27 of max 28 words): Buka Neraca. Neraca menampilkan aset, liabilitas, dan ekuitas per tanggal tertentu. Baris di atas memberi tahu bila neraca seimbang. Bila tidak seimbang, ada jurnal yang perlu diperiksa.
- EN (27 of max 30 words): Open the Neraca. The balance sheet shows assets, liabilities and equity at a given date. The line at the top tells you whether the balance sheet balances.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Lihat Bagan Akun

- Window: 01:21.1 - 01:33.5 (12.4 s)
- ID (24 of max 28 words): Lihat Bagan Akun. Bagan Akun memuat semua akun standar PSAK Indonesia. Cari akun berdasarkan kode atau nama, dan tambahkan akun baru lewat Akun Baru.
- EN (30 of max 31 words): See the Bagan Akun. The chart of accounts holds all standard Indonesian PSAK accounts. Search for an account by code or name, and add a new one with Akun Baru.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Akun bertanda Sistem dan Kontrol dipakai otomatis oleh aplikasi. Jangan diubah tanpa alasan. / Accounts marked Sistem and Kontrol are used automatically by the app. Do not change them without a reason.
