# Narasi / Narration: Deck Presentasi: buat, edit, dan bagikan

Video: `deck-presentasi.5017d691.mp4` (1280x640, 01:37.1, silent). Guide slug: `deck-presentasi`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Buat deck slide untuk pitch atau laporan, isi dengan teks, bagikan lewat tautan atau undangan kolaborator, dan ekspor sebagai PDF.
- EN: Build a slide deck for a pitch or report, fill it with text, share it by link or by inviting collaborators, and export it as a PDF.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka menu Deck Presentasi

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.4 (9.4 s)
- ID (27 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buka menu Deck Presentasi. Setiap deck tampil sebagai kartu dengan status dan jumlah slide. Klik Deck Baru untuk membuat, atau Impor untuk memasukkan presentasi yang sudah ada.
- EN (30 of max 23 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Open the Deck Presentasi menu. Each deck is a card with its status and slide count. Click Deck Baru to create one, or Impor to bring in an existing presentation.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Menu ini terbuka untuk semua peran. / This menu is open to every role.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Buat deck baru

- Window: 00:09.4 - 00:22.8 (13.4 s)
- ID (24 of max 31 words): Buat deck baru. Isi Judul, deskripsi singkat, dan pilih Ukuran Kanvas seperti 16:9 HD. Klik Buat Deck; editor langsung terbuka dengan satu slide kosong.
- EN (29 of max 33 words): Create a new deck. Enter the Judul, a short description, and choose the Ukuran Kanvas such as 16:9 HD. Click Buat Deck; the editor opens with one blank slide.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Kenali editor

- Window: 00:22.8 - 00:31.0 (8.2 s)
- ID (15 of max 18 words): Kenali editor. Bilah alat di atas berisi Text, Shapes, Line, Image, Table, Chart, dan Icon.
- EN (19 of max 20 words): Get to know the editor. The toolbar at the top holds Text, Shapes, Line, Image, Table, Chart and Icon.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Perubahan disimpan otomatis; indikator Tersimpan di bilah atas menunjukkan statusnya. / Changes are saved automatically; the Tersimpan indicator in the top bar shows the state.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: Sisipkan teks

- Window: 00:31.0 - 00:41.1 (10.1 s)
- ID (21 of max 23 words): Sisipkan teks. Klik Text di bilah alat, lalu pilih gaya: Title, Subtitle, Body text, atau Caption. Elemen teks muncul di slide.
- EN (24 of max 25 words): Insert text. Click Text in the toolbar, then pick a style: Title, Subtitle, Body text or Caption. A text element appears on the slide.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Ubah isi teks

- Window: 00:41.1 - 00:53.3 (12.2 s)
- ID (27 of max 28 words): Ubah isi teks. Klik dua kali pada teks di slide, pilih semua dengan Ctrl+A, lalu ketik isi yang baru. Klik di luar teks untuk menutup mode edit.
- EN (26 of max 30 words): Change the text. Double-click the text on the slide, select everything with Ctrl+A, then type the new content. Click outside the text to leave edit mode.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Elemen yang dipilih bisa digeser dengan seret dan diubah ukurannya lewat panel Properti. / A selected element can be moved by dragging and resized in the Properti panel.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Tambah slide dari template

- Window: 00:53.3 - 01:05.5 (12.2 s)
- ID (26 of max 28 words): Tambah slide dari template. Klik Slide Baru untuk slide kosong, atau panah di sebelahnya untuk memilih template seperti Slide Judul, Mood Board, Storyboard, dan Daftar Shot.
- EN (33 of max 30 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Add a slide from a template. Click Slide Baru for a blank slide, or the arrow next to it to choose a template such as Slide Judul, Mood Board, Storyboard and Daftar Shot.

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Bagikan atau ekspor

- Window: 01:05.5 - 01:14.8 (9.4 s)
- ID (18 of max 21 words): Bagikan atau ekspor. Tombol Bagikan membuka pengaturan akses, dan Ekspor mengunduh deck. Tombol Presentasi memulai tayangan layar penuh.
- EN (22 of max 23 words): Share or export. The Bagikan button opens the access settings, and Ekspor downloads the deck. The Presentasi button starts a full-screen slideshow.

## Langkah 8 / Step 8: Atur tautan publik dan kolaborator

- Window: 01:14.8 - 01:26.1 (11.3 s)
- ID (24 of max 26 words): Atur tautan publik dan kolaborator. Di dialog Bagikan deck, nyalakan Tautan publik agar siapa pun yang punya tautan bisa melihat, lalu pilih hak aksesnya.
- EN (27 of max 28 words): Set the public link and collaborators. In the Bagikan deck dialog, turn on Tautan publik so anyone with the link can view, then choose the access level.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Tautan publik bisa dimatikan kapan saja. Deck klien yang dipublikasikan juga tampil di portal klien. / The public link can be switched off at any time. A published client deck also appears in the client portal.

## Langkah 9 / Step 9: Ekspor sebagai PDF

- Window: 01:26.1 - 01:37.1 (10.9 s)
- ID (24 of max 25 words): Ekspor sebagai PDF. Klik Ekspor lalu pilih kualitas PDF: Draf (cepat), Standar, atau Tinggi (lambat). Pilihan terakhir mengekspor slide yang sedang dibuka sebagai PNG.
- EN (26 of max 27 words): Export as PDF. Click Ekspor and choose the PDF quality: Draf (fast), Standar or Tinggi (slow). The last option exports the current slide as a PNG.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Pakai kualitas Draf untuk koreksi internal, dan Tinggi hanya untuk versi final. / Use Draf for internal proofing and Tinggi only for the final version.
