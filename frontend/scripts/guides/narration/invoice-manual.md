# Narasi / Narration: Invoice manual: tagih tanpa penawaran

Video: `invoice-manual.cefd732e.mp4` (1280x900, 01:20.7, silent). Guide slug: `invoice-manual`.

Timestamps are mm:ss.s in the video. Each step has a window (from when the step starts until the next one starts) and a word budget at a calm speaking pace
(Indonesian about 2.4 words/second, English about 2.6). The drafts are written to fit; shorten them or leave pauses rather than speed up.
The orange highlight and numbered badge appear a few seconds into each step, so name the thing the badge points at when it shows.

Pembukaan (opsional, sebelum 00:00) / Opening (optional, before 00:00):

- ID: Buat invoice langsung di Invoice Baru bila tidak ada penawaran yang disetujui: isi klien, proyek, item, PPN, dan informasi pembayaran, perhatikan materai untuk total lebih dari Rp 5.000.000, lalu simpan sebagai draf.
- EN: Create an invoice directly in New Invoice when there is no approved quotation: fill in client, project, items, PPN and payment information, watch the stamp duty (materai) for totals above Rp 5.000.000, then save it as a draft.

## Langkah 1 / Step 1: Buka Invoice Baru

- Window: 00:00.0 - 00:09.3 (9.3 s)
- ID (28 of max 21 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Buka Invoice Baru. Di menu Invoice, klik Invoice Baru. Cara ini untuk menagih tanpa penawaran, misalnya pekerjaan tambahan. Invoice dari penawaran yang disetujui dibuat otomatis, lihat panduan Quotation.
- EN (21 of max 23 words): Open New Invoice. In the Invoice menu click Invoice Baru. Use this to bill without a quotation, for example extra work.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Halaman ini hanya untuk Admin. Tagihan dari penawaran, status, dan pembayarannya dijelaskan di panduan Invoice dan Pembayaran dan piutang. / This page is for Admins only. Invoices from quotations, their statuses and payments are explained in the Invoice and Payments and receivables guides.

## Langkah 2 / Step 2: Pilih klien dan proyek, atur tanggal

- Window: 00:09.3 - 00:24.3 (14.9 s)
- ID (29 of max 34 words): Pilih klien dan proyek, atur tanggal. Pilih Klien, lalu Proyek: proyek hanya muncul setelah klien dipilih, dan keduanya wajib. Jika klien belum ada, pilih + New client di daftar.
- EN (28 of max 37 words): Choose the client and project, set the dates. Choose the Klien (client), then the Proyek (project): projects only appear once a client is chosen, and both are required.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Setelah proyek dipilih, produk proyek itu dan biaya reimburse yang belum ditagihkan otomatis menjadi baris item. Periksa dan ubah seperlunya. / Once the project is chosen, its products and any reimbursable costs not yet billed automatically become item rows. Check and adjust them.

## Langkah 3 / Step 3: Isi baris item

- Window: 00:24.3 - 00:38.8 (14.5 s)
- ID (21 of max 33 words): Isi baris item. Setiap baris berisi nama item atau layanan, catatan opsional, Qty, dan Harga; subtotal baris dan total dihitung otomatis.
- EN (29 of max 36 words): Fill in the item rows. Each row has an item or service name, an optional note, Qty and Harga (price); line subtotals and the total are calculated for you.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Bagian Deskripsi Pekerjaan di bawahnya opsional; bila dikosongkan, ia mewarisi dari proyek. / The Deskripsi Pekerjaan (work description) section below is optional; if left empty it is inherited from the project.

## Langkah 4 / Step 4: PPN dan total

- Window: 00:38.8 - 00:49.3 (10.5 s)
- ID (26 of max 24 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): PPN dan total. Kotak PPN (11%) tercentang secara bawaan; hilangkan centangnya bila invoice tidak memakai PPN. Total adalah subtotal ditambah PPN. 000, sehingga materai belum diperlukan.
- EN (27 of max 26 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): PPN and the total. The PPN (11%) box is ticked by default; untick it if the invoice does not carry PPN. Total is the subtotal plus PPN.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Materai baru diperlukan bila total lebih dari Rp 5.000.000. Total tepat Rp 5.000.000 tidak memerlukannya. / Stamp duty (materai) is only needed when the total is more than Rp 5.000.000. A total of exactly Rp 5.000.000 does not need it.

## Langkah 5 / Step 5: Materai muncul otomatis

- Window: 00:49.3 - 00:57.1 (7.8 s)
- ID (14 of max 17 words): Materai muncul otomatis. 000. 000 pada cetakan sebelum dikirim. Saklar Wajib materai terkunci menyala.
- EN (18 of max 19 words): Materai appears automatically. 000. 000 stamp on the printout before sending. The Wajib materai switch is locked on.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Nilai materai tidak ditambahkan ke total invoice dan penempelannya manual di kertas. Setelah ditempel, centang Materai sudah ditempel saat mengedit invoice, seperti di panduan Invoice. / The stamp value is not added to the invoice total and sticking it is manual, on paper. Once it is stuck, tick Materai sudah ditempel when editing the invoice, as in the Invoice guide.

## Langkah 6 / Step 6: Informasi pembayaran dan syarat

- Window: 00:57.1 - 01:07.7 (10.6 s)
- ID (29 of max 24 words) (OVER BUDGET: trim a few words): Informasi pembayaran dan syarat. Informasi Pembayaran terisi dari rekening yang diatur di Pengaturan; klik Pakai default untuk mengembalikan templatenya. Syarat and Ketentuan sudah berisi teks bawaan; sesuaikan bila perlu.
- EN (24 of max 26 words): Payment information and terms. Informasi Pembayaran (payment information) is filled from the bank accounts set in Settings; click Pakai default to restore the template.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Rekening yang dicetak diatur di Pengaturan, bagian Rekening Bank. / The printed accounts are set in Settings, under Rekening Bank (Bank Accounts).

## Langkah 7 / Step 7: Simpan sebagai draf, lalu kirim

- Window: 01:07.7 - 01:20.7 (13.0 s)
- ID (29 of max 30 words): Simpan sebagai draf, lalu kirim. Klik Simpan di bagian bawah. Invoice dibuat sebagai Draf dengan nomor otomatis, dan belum dikirim ke klien. Periksa isinya, lalu klik Kirim saat siap.
- EN (29 of max 32 words): Save as a draft, then send. Click Simpan at the bottom. The invoice is created as Draf with an automatic number and is not sent to the client yet.
- Tip (opsional / optional, only if there is time): Invoice yang sudah Terkirim tidak bisa kembali ke Draf. Status, pembayaran, dan PDF dijelaskan di panduan Invoice dan Pembayaran dan piutang. / A sent invoice cannot go back to Draf. Statuses, payments and the PDF are explained in the Invoice and Payments and receivables guides.
